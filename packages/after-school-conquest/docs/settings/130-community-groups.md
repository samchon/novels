If a group has no formal representative, the record says so. If a person speaks only personally, the record preserves that scope.

The ledger prevents a fictional community from becoming a faceless resource pool. A request can be declined, redirected, or answered partially. The founders retain unresolved questions and avoid assigning a whole district a single opinion for narrative convenience.
