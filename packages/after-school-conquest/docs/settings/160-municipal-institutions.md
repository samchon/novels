# Municipal Institutions

Haneul city government and Haneul Public High School are separate authorities. This file fixes municipal offices, jurisdiction, service counters, and public records without inventing real legal powers beyond the fictional city’s stated operating decisions.

## City government boundary


**Status:** Work decision — fictional institutional canon.

Haneul city hall owns municipal services, public roads, drainage assets, civic facilities, and city administrative records within the fictional boundary. It does not own the public high-school building, school records, grades, club recognition, or school discipline. The school is under the fictional Haneul Metropolitan Office of Education as fixed in `001` and `150`.

The city and school may coordinate, but a joint meeting does not merge their authority. Every notice names issuer, jurisdiction, effective time, affected place, and contact. A school map may show a city route; it cannot certify that route’s current closure.

## Flood-response office


**Status:** Work decision — inherited authority canon.

The municipal flood-response office owns the yellow flood-level gauge, the city’s closure decision for the affected lower passage and bus-loop ramp, and the public warning route described in `030`. The 1.2-metre threshold closes the specified covered/lower passage and ramp; it does not by itself close every street in Haneul.

The office receives gauge information, field reports, weather information, and infrastructure status. A founder may report an observation and ask for the current official status. A youth-centre coordinator, school adviser, market trader, or volunteer cannot issue or revoke the city closure.

## Public works and drainage maintenance


**Status:** Work decision — municipal service canon.

Public works maintains city-side pumps, drainage inlets, raised electrical cabinets, road surfaces, barriers, and repair schedules within its assignment. The three raised cabinets and pump maintenance are fixed flood legacy, not an invitation to invent pump counts elsewhere. A repair ticket has location, hazard, owner, urgency, and next inspection.

Students may report blocked drains or damaged signs from a safe public position. They do not open a cabinet, enter water, move a barrier, or test an electrical asset. A maintenance plan is not proof that a route is safe during the next storm.

## Roads and traffic office


**Status:** Work decision — jurisdiction boundary.

The municipal roads or traffic office owns ordinary road markings, kerb treatments, traffic control, bus-loop geometry, and approved temporary barriers within city jurisdiction. The office coordinates with transport operators but does not decide school attendance, grades, or access to a school room.

The founders describe a crossing by legible landmarks, direction, date, and observed condition. A faded marking, a driver’s suggestion, or a crowd’s path is not a formal traffic change. Temporary controls show an issuer and expiry where practicable.

## Youth and family services office


**Status:** Work decision — civic-service canon.

The city youth and family services office funds or supervises applicable public programmes and may receive reports from the youth-centre coordinator. It does not own school facilities or city flood closure authority unless a specific municipal delegation says so. A programme invitation names the host, age range, safeguarding contact, and room.

Students can ask for a programme, accessibility support, or referral. A programme worker can stop an unsafe activity and escalate a safeguarding concern. Informal familiarity with a worker does not authorize access to another family’s case record.

## Library and cultural services office


**Status:** Work decision — public-learning canon.

The library and cultural services office administers the public library and permitted civic learning spaces. It sets opening, seat, computer, archive, and event rules for those facilities. It does not certify a school club’s research, and a librarian’s help is not a city policy endorsement.

The founders ask for source discovery, accessible formats, and public-room conditions through the library’s own channel. They respect user privacy and return materials. A public catalogue entry identifies a source, not the truth of every claim copied from it.

## Civil complaint and service request


**Status:** Work decision — process canon.

A city service request records requester, location, observable problem, date, safety concern, requested remedy, and contact preference. A complaint may seek review of conduct or decision; a service ticket seeks maintenance or information. The founders do not collapse them into one anonymous message.

The office provides a receipt, reference, response, or reason for referral where its process permits. Silence is logged as no response by a date, not as a completed repair. Escalation names the next competent office and preserves the original facts rather than exaggerating them.

## Public notice and emergency communication


**Status:** Work decision — municipal communication rule.

An official city notice identifies issuing office, affected area, effective period, action required, access alternative, update channel, and correction contact. A loudspeaker, web page, sign, text, or staff statement may reach different audiences; the city chooses appropriate channels for the risk.

The founders may translate or summarize a notice, but label the version and preserve the source link or reference. A hand-written market note, a forwarded chat, and a city notice are separate records. During immediate danger, people follow the current official instruction rather than waiting for the club’s translation.

## Civic facility booking


**Status:** Work decision — physical-use canon.

A city facility booking states host, room, date, hours, capacity, access route, equipment, cleaning, key or staff handoff, and cancellation conditions. A booking confirms a reservation, not structural safety beyond the facility’s current inspection status.

The youth centre, library, and other public rooms have separate owners. The founders do not move a booking from one building to another by analogy. A flood warning, fire inspection, or staffing absence can suspend a booking; the responsible facility communicates the change.

## City employees and public contact


**Status:** Work decision — authority clarity.

An employee’s badge, uniform, email, or counter position identifies a work relationship but not every city power. The record names department, duty, and whether the statement is an official notice, case-specific response, or informal guidance. A volunteer or contractor can be authorized for a task without being a city decision maker.

The founders speak respectfully and ask for a written route when a decision affects safety, access, or records. They do not harass staff for a preferred answer. A contact leaving the office changes the handoff owner but does not erase the request.

## City-school coordination


**Status:** Work decision — interinstitutional boundary.

Coordination may cover route notices, emergency information, youth programming, accessibility, or shared public space. A coordination note states each institution’s owner, deliverable, date, data boundary, and unresolved issue. A school can distribute a city closure notice without becoming the city’s closure authority.

If the city and school records differ, the founders identify the time and issuer of each and ask the competent office to reconcile them. They do not merge versions into a convenient sentence. The latest statement is current only when its issuing authority and effective period are clear.

## Municipal archive and retention


**Status:** Work decision — records canon.

The city retains public records according to the purpose and applicable administrative rule of the responsible office. A civic archive, service ticket, private notebook, and public webpage have different owners and retention conditions. The founders request only what they need and do not copy personal data as a research shortcut.

An archived notice can explain historical conditions without proving current access. If a page changes, the record identifies capture date and issuer where permitted. A missing archive entry remains an uncertainty, not evidence that an event or closure never existed.
