This package delivers one complete English novel covering the fixed fictional interval March 2027 through February 2031 in twenty numbered volumes. The delivery is not a first volume plus optional sequels: every volume is required, remains in causal order, and receives its own storyline, scenario, and finished manuscript. The school interval is three Korean high-school years (Volumes 1–17); Volumes 18–20 cover the founders’ first year after graduation. The work contains no supernatural system, lethal combat plot, or sexual content involving minors.

Each volume must end with a changed obligation, resource, relationship, or authority that the next volume inherits. A volume may contain local closure, but it cannot erase its predecessor’s ledger, fatigue, public commitments, or institutional memory. The final volume closes the founders’ school-era arc while leaving ordinary civic work unfinished; “complete” means the reader receives a designed ending, not a claim that Haneul has become perfect.
