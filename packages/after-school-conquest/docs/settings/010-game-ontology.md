**Status:** Work decision - fictional civic-maintenance game.

Conquest is the founders' name for a voluntary process in which students help a host maintain one small public-facing condition and receive a dated seal when the host confirms the work. A “conquest” is therefore a completed relationship among a host, a task, a responsible student, evidence, and a review date. The word is theatrical: it describes the founders' exaggerated map language, not a transfer of land, a person, a service, or government authority.

Minseo may draft a proposal; the host or delegated adult must agree before work begins; the assigned student records the task; and the host may cancel it without penalty. The game cannot recruit a student through grades, access to food or transport, romantic pressure, or public embarrassment. Its success condition is maintained usefulness, not a rising score. A proposal that lacks a willing host, safe task, or review date is a map mark only, never a conquest.

The fictional rule is designed around youth voice and voluntary participation. The Convention on the Rights of the Child Article 12 protects a child's opportunity to express views in matters affecting them, with due weight given to age and maturity; it does not authorize the founders to govern other people. Source: [UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 12](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/convention-rights-child#art12).

**Status:** Work decision - fictional spatial boundary.

A territory is a bounded service zone, route, object, or recurring condition that a host can identify without exposing a person's home or private life. Examples are the library's charging shelf, the market's covered notice board, or the accessible route between the bus loop and the school gate. A territory has a name, physical boundary, owner or responsible office, access hours, hazard note, and review date in Ledger Form G-L01.

Only a person with authority over the place may define the boundary. A student may suggest a boundary but cannot draw a neighbour's apartment, a classmate's body, a family circumstance, or a public office into the game without consent. The territory is a description of work, not a deed. Two overlapping territories must name separate tasks or merge into one host-approved record; overlapping seals never create priority.

The map uses a paper grid and an encrypted digital copy with coarse public coordinates. The public version omits private addresses and exact movement patterns. If a route becomes unsafe, the host marks it suspended in red ink and the digital record shows the suspension timestamp; no player may “defend” it by entering a closed space.

**Status:** Work decision - fictional certification artifact.

A seal is a round brass-coloured stamp bearing the club's three-line mark, a serial number, the host initials, and an expiry month. It certifies only that a named task was reported completed and accepted at a particular date. The host signs the matching G-L01 line; the club adviser countersigns when school resources or an external adult are involved. A seal has no cash value, grade value, legal authority, or ownership effect.

The physical seal stays in the ledger sleeve rather than on a person or building. A digital scan is a convenience copy and cannot replace the host signature. A missing serial, altered date, or absent host confirmation makes the seal void pending reconstruction. The club may publish a count of valid seals, but not a name or photograph merely because a seal exists. The stamp is an invitation to inspect maintenance, not a medal that makes the holder superior.

**Status:** Work decision - fictional noncash labour record.

A token is a paper square printed in denominations of one, two, and four completed volunteer-hours. It is a non-transferable acknowledgment of time already worked; it is not money, wages, academic credit, a raffle ticket, a material-donation receipt, an expense authorization, or a claim on another person. The treasurer issues it only against a dated shift sheet, and the worker signs whether the record is accurate. Material donations, printing costs, and emergency transport belong to receipts and the separate budget ledger, not to tokens.

Tokens cannot be spent, traded, pooled, redeemed, or used to obtain grades, favours, food, transport, or access. They may be corrected or cancelled only by the treasurer and adviser together, with the original serial and reason retained. The public display cap is forty hours per student so the map cannot create a prestige ranking; hours above forty remain in the private provenance record and appear only in an anonymous aggregate. The cap does not delete, transfer, or confiscate labour.

Counterfeit or duplicated tokens are quarantined, not publicly mocked. The treasurer and adviser compare the shift sheet to the ledger within three school days. A proven error receives a correction or cancellation mark; the completed labour remains historical evidence and no fictional cash is created.

**Status:** Work decision - fictional provenance record.

Every conquest receives a G-L01 ledger identity composed of territory code, sequential task number, proposer initials, host category, creation date, responsible student, consent mode, resource estimate, risk level, review date, and closure reason. Names are optional unless necessary to contact a host; a student ID is never copied into the public map. The paper master is kept in the locked audiovisual booth cabinet, and the encrypted digital copy has two-person access.

Minseo can create a draft number; only the secretary can activate it; the host can amend the task; the adviser can freeze it; and the records officer can authorize deletion of a temporary contact field. Every edit appends a timestamp and editor rather than overwriting history. The ledger is not a school attendance register, health file, voter list, or municipal database. If paper and digital records disagree, the most recent witnessed host entry governs while the discrepancy remains open.

**Status:** Work decision - voluntary and informed participation rule.

Consent requires a plain-language explanation of task, location, time, burden, foreseeable risk, data collected, publicity, review date, and withdrawal method. The host chooses written, recorded verbal, or accessible digital confirmation; the student reads back the operative task before signing. A guardian or adult signature is added when school policy or the host's service requires it, but it does not erase the young person's right to understand and refuse.

Silence, a like, a crowd cheer, prior participation, or possession of a token is not consent. The club asks separately for work consent, data consent, and publicity consent. A host may approve a repaired shelf while refusing a photograph. The club records “declined” without a reason unless the person volunteers one, and the refusal produces no negative score or public label.

The consent practice is a work decision informed by the youth-participation principle in CRC Article 12 and the privacy principles of purpose limitation and data minimization in Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. **Status:** Work decision - enforceable exit rule.

Any host, student, volunteer, adviser, or affected passer-by may say “no” before work begins or “stop” during it. The stop is entered on the task card with time, person category, immediate safety action, and whether follow-up is invited. A student who leaves a voluntary shift keeps membership, existing credit for work actually completed, and access to the club's ordinary information. No founder may ask a teacher, parent, council, or host to punish refusal.

The only exception is an immediate danger in which an adult or emergency service directs people away; that direction is safety action, not game consent. A host may later invite a revised proposal, but the club cannot reopen a declined task until the host initiates contact. **Status:** Work decision - post-completion duty.

A seal creates a maintenance duty rather than a permanent victory. G-L01 names the next check, responsible shift, material reserve, access key, and host contact route. A low-risk shelf receives a seven-day check; an outdoor notice board receives a weather check after heavy rain; a route observation is revisited only during agreed access hours. These intervals are fictional operating values, not universal safety standards.

The responsible student may delegate a shift but remains responsible for reporting the handoff. The host may maintain the condition alone and close the club's duty. **Status:** Work decision - dated validity limit.

A seal expires at 23:59 on its printed review date, immediately when the host withdraws consent, or immediately when the task's physical condition changes enough that the original description is false. The ledger records the first applicable reason. Expiry removes only the current public map mark; completed-time tokens remain immutable provenance records. Expiry does not erase the historical record or blame the host for ordinary change.

The secretary sends an accessible reminder seven days before expiry. Renewal requires a fresh host confirmation and a new task condition; copying the old stamp is prohibited. A weather closure, school closure, or absent host pauses the countdown only when an adult records the reason and a new date. Expiry ends the seal and public map validity, never the historical record of completed labour, a token's provenance, a receipt, or a correction. It is a normal form of care: the club would rather have an honest blank map than a false promise.

**Status:** Work decision - staged disagreement record.

A disputed conquest receives one of four statuses: questioned, paused, under review, or resolved. “Questioned” preserves activity while a factual detail is checked; “paused” stops work for safety or consent; “under review” convenes host, student, and adviser; “resolved” records correction, withdrawal, or confirmation. The secretary opens G-D01 within one school day of a complaint and sends an accessible receipt to the affected parties.

The reviewer cannot be the person who made the disputed entry. A host can request correction without debating the club's public score. Review compares task card, witness statement, receipt, and physical condition; it does not require a person to disclose private history. The final record states who decided, what evidence was considered, what remains uncertain, and where an institutional appeal goes. Harassment, retaliation, and doxxing are separate safeguarding or discipline matters, not game disputes.

**Status:** Work decision - absolute semantic boundary.

No conquest, seal, token, map mark, or ledger entry transfers ownership of land, a person, a student organization, a public service, a cultural practice, or government power. The city retains municipal authority over city property; the education office retains authority over Haneul Public High School; a host retains authority over a hosted task. The club cannot rename a place's legal name or speak for a resident who has not authorized it.

The prohibition also covers symbolic possession. A student may not call a nonparticipant “enemy,” display a refusal as a conquered gap, or demand loyalty because the club repaired something. Public language uses “maintained,” “paused,” or “invited,” never “owned.” Any map that implies possession is withdrawn, corrected, and shown to the host before republication.

**Status:** Work decision - relationship to real institutions.

Conquest is a fictional school game operating inside an ordinary contemporary world. It cannot override the Korean school system, the 640-student public school's education-office supervision, municipal permits, privacy law, emergency instructions, building safety, transport rules, or a person's right to leave. The Haneul Metropolitan Office of Education and Haneul city government are separate authorities; neither becomes subordinate to the club's map.

The founders can observe, ask, repair with permission, document, and hand work back. They cannot issue fines, inspect private records, certify engineering, provide medical treatment, compel attendance, or buy public assets without the responsible institution's process. When game language conflicts with a real safety instruction, the safety instruction wins and the adviser records the interruption. This boundary keeps comedy aimed at the founders' imagination while making every civic gain materially accountable.
