**Status:** Source-backed baseline plus work decision for fictional application.

The Korean Elementary and Secondary Education Act Article 24, effective 2026-08-20, and the Enforcement Decree Article 44, effective 2026-05-26, are the current official baselines consulted for school operation and semester structure: [Act Article 24](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1032276043) and [Enforcement Decree Article 44](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1002280340). Their legal scope is distinguished from Haneul’s fictional dates and school offices in `030`.

The novel does not quote a legal baseline as permission for a student to enter a room, publish a record, or override a closure. A current statute is a source for a legal proposition; a fictional work decision is the operative detail when Haneul supplies a named office, date, threshold, or route.

**Status:** Work decision — fictional record canon.

Haneul records are separated into instructional records, attendance and examination records, student-support or safeguarding records, facilities and booking records, club working records, and public notices. Each category has an owner, purpose, access audience, correction route, retention condition, and disclosure limit. A student’s personal notebook is not silently promoted to a school record.

The founders request the category and owner before asking for contents. A public notice may be shared widely; a support record may require restricted handling. A room booking can show reservation status but not a student’s private reason for requesting it.

## Data minimization and purpose


**Status:** Work decision — privacy rule.

The club collects the minimum information needed for a stated activity: names or contact routes, consent scope, access function, equipment, time, and responsible host. It does not collect diagnoses, immigration status, family gossip, precise home addresses, or popularity data for convenience.

Before collection, the holder explains purpose, audience, retention, correction, and deletion or archival route. A future research use is a new purpose requiring a new decision. A spreadsheet is not neutral merely because it is private on a laptop.

## Correction and contested record


**Status:** Work decision — administrative process.

A person may identify a factual error, missing context, wrong name, stale route, or misattributed quote. The record owner states how to request correction, what can be amended, what must remain as historical version, and who reviews a dispute. The founders do not overwrite a record so that the correction disappears.

Where two accounts conflict, both are labelled by source and date until the competent owner decides. A correction notice reaches the same audience as the error when feasible. The club records that a request was made and its status without publishing sensitive details.

## Consent and minor participation


**Status:** Work decision — safeguarding boundary.

Consent requirements vary by activity, risk, data, and host. A student’s willingness to attend does not automatically authorize image publication, public quotation, travel, equipment use, or disclosure of family information. The host names any required guardian or school process.

The founders explain risks in language the participant can understand and provide a refusal route. A guardian’s permission does not authorize the club to ignore the student’s expressed discomfort. A school form does not authorize an unrelated civic publication.

## Accessibility legal reference and fictional design


**Status:** Source-backed baseline plus work decision.

The official Accessibility Act and its Enforcement Rule Appendix 1 provide technical and legal reference points used in `050`, including route, door, lift, ramp, and toilet dimensions: [Act Article 3](https://www.law.go.kr/법령/장애인ㆍ노인ㆍ임산부등의편의증진보장에관한법률#제3조), [Act Article 8](https://www.law.go.kr/법령/장애인ㆍ노인ㆍ임산부등의편의증진보장에관한법률#제8조), and [official Appendix 1 PDF](https://law.go.kr/LSW/flDownload.do?bylClsCd=110201&flSeq=135226913). The appendix is a technical standard with its own applicability; it is not a promise that every fictional existing building already complies.

Haneul’s corridor, lift, ramp, and room dimensions are work decisions aligned to the physical envelope, while an inspection owner decides actual usability. A lawful minimum and a generous design are not the same claim.

## Public information and restricted information


**Status:** Work decision — information boundary.

Public information may include a published timetable, closure notice, public budget, or approved meeting record. Restricted information may include student support, contact details, keys, security layouts, private family circumstances, or an incident involving a minor. A document’s presence in a public building does not make every field public.

The founders ask for a redacted or aggregate version when individual detail is unnecessary. They do not infer a person’s condition from a redaction. A public-interest reason must be specific and proportionate to the disclosure requested.

## Images, audio, and location data


**Status:** Work decision — media canon.

Recording has a purpose, subject, device, storage owner, access list, and deletion or correction path. A face, voice, uniform, route, timestamp, or window view can identify a person or home. A city street is public space, not blanket permission to publish every person in it.

The founders seek applicable host consent, avoid recording confidential conversations, and disclose when a recording is being made. A blurred image can still reveal a school, disability aid, or routine. Location precision is reduced unless the exact point is necessary and authorized.

## Copyright, quotation, and attribution


**Status:** Work decision — research practice.

The club distinguishes its own map, a public-domain or licensed source, a quotation, a paraphrase, and a screenshot. A source record includes title, issuer or author, date, locator, access date, and the claim supported. A copied paragraph is not transformed into club knowledge by changing its font.

The English manuscript may quote briefly for a defined purpose and otherwise paraphrases with attribution. A public webpage can change; the record preserves the accessed title and date where permitted. A school logo or city seal is not used as personal endorsement.

## Contracts, grants, and institutional promises


**Status:** Work decision — authority canon.

A grant, donation, room booking, service order, or partnership has issuer, recipient, scope, amount or in-kind value, term, reporting duty, cancellation, and signature or approval method. A student’s enthusiastic email is not a contract unless the competent institution accepts it through the applicable process.

The founders read conditions before promising public outcomes. Restricted funds remain restricted; donated equipment retains its owner until transferred. A verbal “we can probably help” is recorded as exploratory and not spent against.

## Appeals and escalation


**Status:** Work decision — procedural access.

An appeal identifies the decision, issuer, date, affected right or resource, grounds, requested remedy, deadline, and receiving authority. A request for explanation is not always an appeal, and a complaint about conduct is not always a request to reverse a decision. The founders use the institution’s named route where one exists.

An appeal does not automatically suspend a safety closure, key restriction, exam rule, or safeguarding action. The record states whether an interim measure exists. A student can ask an adult for help without surrendering authorship of the account.

## Legal uncertainty and fictional law


**Status:** Work decision — uncertainty protocol.

When a real legal question matters, the owner consults a current official source and records title, article, effective date, and scope. If the source is unavailable, ambiguous, or not applicable to a fictional fact, the setting states a work decision rather than pretending certainty. The source URL is not itself a legal conclusion.

The package never invents a court ruling, statutory penalty, or public office power to force a plot solution. A later layer can contain a character’s mistaken legal belief, but the settings record retains the actual adopted rule and its owner.
