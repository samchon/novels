# Student Social Life

This file fixes the ordinary social systems that surround schoolwork: clubs, study, meals, movement, competition, leisure, and reputation. It keeps social life from becoming a single popularity hierarchy or an invisible plot reward.

**Status:** Work decision — social information rule.

Reputation is a circulating interpretation, not a reliable record. A student may be known for punctuality, jokes, translation, repair, absence, or a rumour that is false. The founders verify claims with the person or competent record owner and avoid forwarding private detail.
