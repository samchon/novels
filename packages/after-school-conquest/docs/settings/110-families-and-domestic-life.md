# Families and Domestic Life

Home is a resource system, not a single emotional backdrop. This file fixes how domestic time, money, language, care, privacy, and housing constrain the founders without dictating family plot. It allows varied households and keeps family members from becoming exposition devices.

## Household forms and naming

**Status:** Work decision — fictional domestic canon.

The founders may live with two parents, one parent, grandparents, siblings, guardians, host relatives, or another lawful caregiving arrangement. “Family” means the people a character names as family; household membership, legal guardianship, emotional closeness, and emergency contact are separate fields. A school form may require one field while a lived household contains several people.

Names are used as the person requests. A household's surname, address, language, or national origin does not establish its internal authority or income. The novel does not treat a single household as representative of all Korean or migrant families.

## Domestic time budget

**Status:** Work decision — executable resource.

Each founder has a weekly domestic budget containing sleep, meals, hygiene, commute preparation, chores, family care, paid work where applicable, study, and unassigned recovery. A club request consumes real minutes and may collide with a parent shift, a sibling pickup, a medical appointment, prayer, rest, or household noise restrictions.

The ledger uses a range when the schedule changes. A promise after school is not free merely because classes have ended. When a home obligation changes, the founder may shorten, delegate, reschedule, or refuse the project. No setting assumes a caregiver can appear on demand.

## Meals, water, and food access

**Status:** Work decision — material canon.

The founders have different lunch routines: school meal, packed food, bought food, skipped food, or a meal prepared at home. Food preference, allergy, religion, income, and sensory comfort are not interchangeable. A public invitation states whether food is provided, optional, labelled, and safe to decline.

Water and a place to eat are operational needs during long tasks. Sharing food can be generous without being compulsory. The club never uses hunger as a test of commitment, asks a peer to explain a family budget, or treats a donated meal as permission to publish a person's image.

## Care of siblings and elders

**Status:** Work decision — care boundary.

A founder may supervise a younger sibling, assist an elder, translate an appointment, walk a relative to transit, or manage a household task. The obligation can be regular, sudden, paid, unpaid, chosen, or imposed. It is recorded as a real constraint without assigning the child legal adult authority that they do not possess.

Club plans include a cancellation path when care starts early or runs late. They do not ask a minor to leave a dependent alone, carry hazardous equipment while caregiving, or disclose private health information to justify absence. An adult referral is required when a household situation creates immediate danger.

## Household language and translation

**Status:** Work decision — language canon.

A home may use Korean, English, Spanish, another language, mixed speech, text, gesture, or different registers with different relatives. Lila's English-language narration does not flatten those differences. A family member's preference for one language is not proof of fluency in another.

Translating a school notice at home is labour. The founder explains whether the document is an official decision or a personal summary and preserves deadlines, fees, safety conditions, and appeal routes. No student is required to translate confidential disciplinary, immigration, medical, or financial material without an appropriate adult or service.

## Money and household contribution

**Status:** Work decision — domestic economy.

Household money may be shared, separated, cash-based, banked, borrowed, or controlled by one adult. A founder's allowance, wages, scholarship, transit card, and club reimbursement are distinct resources. A request for printing, fare, food, or equipment names who pays and whether repayment is expected.

Poverty is not a personality trait or a plot twist. A family can be financially strained while generous, or financially secure while imposing strict limits. The founders do not reveal a household's income in public to win sympathy. A no-cost alternative is designed as a normal option, not a consolation.

## Housing, privacy, and work surfaces

**Status:** Work decision — physical domestic canon.

Homes vary in room count, shared bedrooms, building access, noise insulation, internet reliability, and safe surfaces for maps or devices. A founder may have no private desk, may work at a kitchen table, or may need to keep school materials out of a sibling's reach. These conditions affect what can be stored and when.

An address, door code, family document, or view into a bedroom is private. Club work uses public school or civic space when home conditions cannot safely support it. A photographed map must not reveal a household's exact location without a specific reason and permission.

## Domestic technology and connectivity

**Status:** Work decision — access canon.

A household may share one phone, use prepaid data, restrict apps, lose power, or rely on public Wi-Fi. Charging a device is a resource with time, outlet safety, and competing users. A group message sent at school may not reach a founder at home until later.

Digital access never proves availability or consent. The founders offer paper, voice, or in-person alternatives where feasible, and mark which channel is authoritative. A family member who answers a phone is not automatically the student who owns the account.

## Curfew, transport, and return duty

**Status:** Work decision — domestic safety rule.

Each household may set a return time, check-in message, route restriction, or companion requirement. The rule can tighten during storms, darkness, illness, work shifts, or public warnings. A school dismissal time does not override a household's safety plan or a city closure.

An outing record includes departure, expected return, route, contact, and contingency. If transport fails, the founder contacts the household or designated adult rather than improvising an unsafe shortcut. No one is shamed for leaving a task to get home.

## Conflict and household authority

**Status:** Work decision — domestic power boundary.

Family disagreement can concern time, money, language, privacy, grades, technology, or safety. A founder may negotiate, ask another trusted adult for help, or decline a task. The setting does not declare every parental instruction wise, nor does it make a teenager the household's sole reformer.

If there is immediate violence, coercion, abandonment, or medical danger, the founder uses an appropriate adult or emergency pathway. The club does not conduct an amateur investigation. A household conflict remains a private matter unless disclosure is authorized or safety duties require referral.

## Emergency contact and medical information

**Status:** Work decision — safeguarding canon.

An emergency contact is the person authorized to receive a specific urgent message; they are not necessarily the guardian, nearest relative, or favourite adult. The school and activity host keep only the information required by their process. A founder can state a functional need—“I need medication at this time”—without sharing a diagnosis.

Emergency instructions identify location, responsible adult, access route, and handoff. Peers do not administer medication, transport an injured person, or publish a health incident unless authorized. Family wishes matter, but immediate safety is handled by the responsible institution and emergency services.

## Domestic memory and objects

**Status:** Work decision — material continuity.

Photographs, recipes, tools, uniforms, letters, religious objects, and inherited furniture can carry family meaning without becoming public evidence. A founder may use a household object for a project only after asking the owner and recording return condition. Damage is reported honestly, including when replacement is difficult.

A family story is told by the person who owns the memory, not automatically by the most articulate child. The setting allows disagreement about what an object means and leaves some histories unrecorded. Domestic continuity is shown through maintenance, not a compulsory archive.

## Leaving home for alumni work

**Status:** Work decision — post-graduation domestic transition.

After graduation, a founder may commute from the same household, move, work irregular hours, attend further education, or remain available for family care. Alumni status changes school access but does not erase domestic obligations. A civic or school request must still state the time, transport, and return burden.

Adult independence is gradual and unequal. The setting does not treat a first job, university offer, or move as automatic freedom from family, nor does it make dependence a failure. Each post-graduation arrangement has its own contact, money, housing, and safety boundary.
