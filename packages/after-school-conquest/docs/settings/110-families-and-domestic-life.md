# Families and Domestic Life

Home is a resource system, not a single emotional backdrop. This file fixes how domestic time, money, language, care, privacy, and housing constrain the founders without dictating family plot. It allows varied households and keeps family members from becoming exposition devices.

**Status:** Work decision — care boundary.

A founder may supervise a younger sibling, assist an elder, translate an appointment, walk a relative to transit, or manage a household task. The obligation can be regular, sudden, paid, unpaid, chosen, or imposed. It is recorded as a real constraint without assigning the child legal adult authority that they do not possess.

**Status:** Work decision — domestic economy.

Household money may be shared, separated, cash-based, banked, borrowed, or controlled by one adult. A founder's allowance, wages, scholarship, transit card, and club reimbursement are distinct resources. **Status:** Work decision — domestic safety rule.

Each household may set a return time, check-in message, route restriction, or companion requirement. The rule can tighten during storms, darkness, illness, work shifts, or public warnings. A school dismissal time does not override a household's safety plan or a city closure.

**Status:** Work decision — domestic power boundary.

Family disagreement can concern time, money, language, privacy, grades, technology, or safety. A founder may negotiate, ask another trusted adult for help, or decline a task. The setting does not declare every parental instruction wise, nor does it make a teenager the household's sole reformer.

**Status:** Work decision — safeguarding canon.

An emergency contact is the person authorized to receive a specific urgent message; they are not necessarily the guardian, nearest relative, or favourite adult. The school and activity host keep only the information required by their process. A founder can state a functional need—“I need medication at this time”—without sharing a diagnosis.

Family wishes matter, but immediate safety is handled by the responsible institution and emergency services.
