**Status:** Work decision — material continuity.

Photographs, recipes, tools, uniforms, letters, religious objects, and inherited furniture can carry family meaning without becoming public evidence. A founder may use a household object for a project only after asking the owner and recording return condition. Damage is reported honestly, including when replacement is difficult.

A family story is told by the person who owns the memory, not automatically by the most articulate child. The setting allows disagreement about what an object means and leaves some histories unrecorded. Domestic continuity is shown through maintenance, not a compulsory archive.

**Status:** Work decision — post-graduation domestic transition.

After graduation, a founder may commute from the same household, move, work irregular hours, attend further education, or remain available for family care. Alumni status changes school access but does not erase domestic obligations. A civic or school request must still state the time, transport, and return burden.

Adult independence is gradual and unequal. The setting does not treat a first job, university offer, or move as automatic freedom from family, nor does it make dependence a failure. Each post-graduation arrangement has its own contact, money, housing, and safety boundary.
