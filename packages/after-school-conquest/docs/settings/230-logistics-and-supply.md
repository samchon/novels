# Logistics and Supply

This file fixes how people, objects, information, and waste move through Haneul. It supplements the physical routes in `050` and `060` without changing their geometry or assigning events.

## Request-to-delivery chain

**Status:** Work decision — logistics canon.

Every supply request names item, quantity, specification, destination, access window, owner, urgency, and cancellation condition. The chain then records approval, purchase or source, pickup or delivery, receipt, inspection, storage, issue, return, and disposal.

The person who requests an item is not automatically the person who may accept it. A delivery can arrive at a school gate, service lane, office counter, market stall, or public facility only under that host’s access rule. A lost handoff is reported rather than reconstructed from hope.

## School receiving boundary

**Status:** Work decision — inherited institutional boundary.

School deliveries use the designated gate, service lane, receiving office, or facilities process. Students do not direct a courier through a locked wing, leave a parcel in a stairwell, or sign for restricted equipment without authority. The school office records recipient, time, condition, and storage.

The west annex and audiovisual booth retain the fire and key rules in `030` and `050`. A package labelled “club” does not bypass inspection, adult-present charging, or fire-rated archive separation.

## Civic receiving boundary

**Status:** Work decision — municipal interface.

City facilities, the youth centre, library, market edge, and public service lane have separate hosts and hours. A delivery addressed to one facility cannot be left in another. The youth-centre coordinator controls centre-side receipt; the municipal flood-response office controls closure status, not the courier’s convenience.

During a warning, no volunteer enters the channel or basement to retrieve a parcel. The supplier reschedules, uses the authorized detour, or returns the item. A city barrier is not moved for a delivery.

## Packaging and accessibility

**Status:** Work decision — material canon.

Packaging is handled so that labels can be read, weights carried, waste removed, and a person using a mobility aid can approach without losing the route. Heavy or unstable items are not assigned to a student merely because they are available.

The group identifies scissors, tape, batteries, liquid, sharp edges, allergens, and disposal needs before delivery. A box cannot be stored in a corridor, lift landing, stair, accessible toilet, or fire path. Packaging itself can become a trip, glare, or waste hazard.

## Inventory and condition

**Status:** Work decision — asset ledger.

Inventory records asset or consumable name, quantity, owner, location, condition, serial or identifier when necessary, custodian, inspection date, and disposal route. “One box” is not enough when contents, expiry, or accessories affect safety.

The founders check received material against order and report damage before use. A working device may still have missing safety documentation or account ownership. Inventory is updated at issue, return, transfer, and loss.

## Shared tools and checkout

**Status:** Work decision — access canon.

A checkout entry states borrower, item, purpose, start, return, condition, accessories, and supervision. Shared tools are stored where the host can monitor them; students do not duplicate keys or passwords to make checkout easier.

The custodian may refuse a request when training, age, weather, room, or safety conditions are unsuitable. A returned item is inspected and any defect isolated. Responsibility follows the recorded handoff, not the person who last remembers seeing it.

## Food, water, and temperature-sensitive items

**Status:** Work decision — health boundary.

Food and temperature-sensitive supplies carry source, date, storage condition, allergen information where available, and discard rule. A public room may not have a refrigerator, sink, or safe preparation surface. The host states what participants may bring and what must remain sealed.

The founders do not diagnose an allergy, store medication casually, or serve unlabelled food to force hospitality. Water access and rest are planned alongside delivery. A hot day or delayed bus can make a safe package unsafe to consume.

## Printing, paper, and archive

**Status:** Work decision — information logistics.

Printed notices state issuer, date, audience, version, contact, and removal or correction condition. A map printed at a different scale is marked. Sensitive drafts are collected or destroyed through the owner’s process rather than placed in a public recycling bin.

Paper archives are stored dry, above flood risk, and separate from batteries or heat sources as fixed in the 2022 fire legacy. A volunteer does not relocate the fire-rated cabinet. A scan is a copy with its own access risk, not an automatic replacement for an official original.

## Waste and reverse logistics

**Status:** Work decision — environmental canon.

The plan accounts for cardboard, plastic, food, batteries, toner, broken tools, contaminated material, and reusable packaging. Disposal follows the host’s rule and the item’s hazard. Batteries do not enter ordinary paper archive or an unlabelled bin.

The person who brings material home is not automatically responsible for permanent disposal. The owner names the return route, collection date, and evidence of transfer where needed. Leaving waste beside a city drain or market passage is never a neutral shortcut.

## Weather and route contingency

**Status:** Work decision — cross-system canon.

Rain, heat, cold, darkness, flood warning, bus delay, crowding, or a closed passage can interrupt delivery. The contingency states a safe alternate, authority to cancel, storage limit, communication route, and rescheduling owner. `070` supplies environmental states and `030` supplies closure authority.

An alternate route must be checked for actual access, not selected from a map alone. When no safe route remains, the package waits. The founders do not reward a courier or student for crossing water, climbing a barrier, or carrying a load beyond capacity.

## Delivery evidence and dispute

**Status:** Work decision — records canon.

Delivery evidence may be a receipt, signed handoff, time-stamped message, condition photograph, inventory entry, or named witness, depending on the item and host. Evidence records what it can prove and what it cannot. A photo of a parcel outside a door does not prove acceptance.

When sender and recipient disagree, the owner isolates the disputed item, checks records, and chooses replacement, return, inspection, or an unresolved entry. The group avoids public blame while preserving a factual chain. A missing signature is not forged after the fact.

## Handoff at graduation and project closure

**Status:** Work decision — continuity canon.

Before graduation or closure, the club lists assets, consumables, orders, subscriptions, open repairs, stored media, borrowed items, and next owners. School-owned supplies return to the school process; civic property returns to its host; personal items return to their people.

An alumni continuation needs a current host and new logistics ledger. A former student cannot keep a school key, confidential list, or unrestricted club account as a souvenir. Closure is complete only when physical and digital supply chains have owners.
