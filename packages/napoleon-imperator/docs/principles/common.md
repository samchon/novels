# Napoleon Imperator Historical Principles

Work-specific historical-integrity criteria for every settings, storyline, scenario, and manuscript file. Settings own direct verification; storylines apply the verified canon; scenarios and manuscripts preserve it without counterfeit precision or quotation. Downstream files inherit research through the evidence graph rather than repeat a research dossier, and any newly needed historical fact is repaired in settings first. These principles check the complete selected file; individual facts and narrative units remain in their authored owners.

## Historical claim provenance {#historical-claim-provenance}

**Authority:** User-confirmed requirement, implemented for this English-language work on 2026-08-21. Every externally verifiable claim in the file retains the identity of the evidence that supports it: source kind, creator or institution, date, and the edition, archival item, page, paragraph, table, map, or other exact locator needed to retrieve it. Distinguish contemporary orders, letters, reports, registers, and objects from official bulletins or commissioned histories, retrospective memoirs, later scholarship, and inherited summaries. Record relevant audience, purpose, revision, translation, and temporal distance instead of treating proximity to the event as automatic truth. A narrative file may inherit this retrievability through its cited settings owners rather than expose research notes or footnotes to the eventual reader, but the authoring graph must preserve the chain.

One source does not inherit another source's authority. A source bundle supports only the claims each cited item actually contains, and a portal, collection page, or modern battlefield caption is not silently promoted into a primary record. **Success:** a reader can tell who asserted each material fact, when and for what kind of record, and can retrieve the supporting passage. **Failure:** misattributing one author's reconstruction to another witness, citing a whole collection for a precise assertion, or laundering an official victory narrative into an unqualified neutral account.

Sources: [US National Archives on identifying retrievable records, items, dates, authors, and recipients](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/citation.html), [W3C PROV-O on entities, activities, agents, derivation, and attribution](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), [Fondation Napoléon on the commissioned, corrected, delayed, and later published official Austerlitz description](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/articles/napoleonic-pages-the-official-description-of-the-battle-of-austerlitz-according-to-napoleons-instructions/)

## Corroboration and disputed standing {#historical-corroboration}

**Authority:** User-confirmed requirement, implemented for this English-language work on 2026-08-21. Source, contextualize, and corroborate material claims before settling them. Preserve disagreement when accounts identify different clocks, places, formations, quantities, causes, participants, or outcomes. Do not fuse observations that name different events into one synthetic timestamp, quote, route, or sequence. Check names, ranks, nationalities, unit affiliations, offices, dates, measurements, and translations against the most direct available records and a competent critical account where the distinction matters.

The file states what is verified, multiply supported, singly attested, disputed, inferred, or chosen for the work. It may adopt a bounded work decision when narrative execution requires one, but it does not relabel that choice as historical consensus. **Success:** competing accounts remain individually attributable and the adopted canon exposes its support and uncertainty. **Failure:** silently averaging incompatible testimony, selecting the most vivid version because it is convenient, or leaving a known contradiction hidden inside confident prose.

Sources: [Stanford Graduate School of Education on sourcing, contextualizing, and cross-checking historical documents](https://ed.stanford.edu/news/stanford-developed-history-lessons-grades-6-12-adopted-worldwide), [Saxton on distinguishing accuracy and authenticity in historical fiction](https://doi.org/10.1080/13642529.2020.1727189), [Fondation Napoléon on the production history of the official Austerlitz account](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/articles/napoleonic-pages-the-official-description-of-the-battle-of-austerlitz-according-to-napoleons-instructions/)

## Bounded historical invention {#bounded-historical-invention}

**Authority:** User-confirmed requirement, implemented for this English-language work on 2026-08-21. Historical gaps permit fiction, not counterfeit evidence. Settings mark invented or composite facts as work decisions. Narrative layers may create dialogue, private gesture, sensory texture, connective action, and inward experience within their declared access, but must not present them as surviving quotation, witnessed thought, or documented causation. An invented bridge remains compatible with established chronology, material conditions, agency, and consequence, and it does not erase a recorded actor merely to simplify Napoleon's path.

Precision stops where support stops. Do not invent an exact minute, distance, casualty count, physical mechanism, order wording, motive, nationality, or formation assignment because specificity sounds authoritative. Reduce the claim to a supported range, preserve alternatives, or leave the matter unresolved. **Success:** readers can inhabit a fully realized scene without being deceived about what the record establishes. **Failure:** hindsight masquerading as a contemporary mind, fabricated certainty filling archival silence, or a plausible detail contradicting the evidence that bounds it.

Sources: [Stanford Graduate School of Education on evaluating a source in context and against other records](https://ed.stanford.edu/news/college-students-struggle-make-sense-evidence-past-stanford-researchers-find), [Cambridge scholarship on documented elements, invention, and constrained plausibility in historical novels](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75), [Saxton on factual accuracy and constructed authenticity](https://doi.org/10.1080/13642529.2020.1727189)
