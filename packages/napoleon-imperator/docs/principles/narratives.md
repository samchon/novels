# Napoleon Imperator Narrative Principles

Work-specific narrative choices repeated across every storyline, scenario, and manuscript file. Historical facts, volume allocation, and scene events remain in their canonical settings and narrative owners.

## Narrator and focal access {#narrator-focal-access}

**Authority:** Work decision — adopted from the user-supplied reference for this English-language work on 2026-08-21. The default mode is third-person limited historical narration. Each scene attaches access to an acting participant, witness, or recipient of a document who is available there. The narration does not claim monopoly over Napoleon's interior life, and it infers another person's intention only as far as speech, action, writing, silence, material trace, and result permit.

Distance may tighten or widen with danger, responsibility, and the scale of action, but a transition gives the reader enough evidence to identify whose perceptions and knowledge now govern. **Success:** the reader can distinguish observed fact, focal interpretation, and narratorial framing while remaining immersed. **Failure:** writing a future result as knowledge already possessed, certifying an opponent's intention through Napoleon's explanation, or combining several characters' private knowledge inside one unmarked passage.

Sources: [Gérard Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, on voice, focalization, and access](https://archive.org/details/narrativediscour0000gene), [the Living Handbook of Narratology on narrator and narrative authority](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/44.html)

## Tense and documentary mediation {#tense-documentary-mediation}

**Authority:** Work decision — adopted from the user-supplied reference for this English-language work on 2026-08-21. Storyline treatments and manuscript narration default to past tense. Scenario action blocks use the immediate present where the production-capable script form requires it; this functional change does not turn later knowledge into contemporary knowledge. Contemporary record, later recollection, scholarly interpretation, official self-defence, and fictional reconstruction remain distinct in sentence stance and document framing. Uncertain causation appears as bounded inference, competing explanation, or observable result rather than retrospective omniscience.

Letters, proclamations, bulletins, testimony, orders, and reports enter as voices produced under particular power, purpose, audience, and time. Quotation or paraphrase makes that mediation legible instead of using a document as an objective camera. This item governs how narrative represents the source layers whose factual identity is checked by `principles/common.md#historical-claim-provenance`. **Failure:** turning a victor's bulletin into neutral narration, treating the record's silence as consent, or letting a later memoir speak with unmarked contemporaneous authority.

Sources: [Fondation Napoléon on Napoleon's instructions, proof corrections, and the unpublished official Austerlitz project](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/articles/napoleonic-pages-the-official-description-of-the-battle-of-austerlitz-according-to-napoleons-instructions/), [Cambridge scholarship on the historical novel's documented and invented epistemologies](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75)

## English prose dialogue and material image {#english-prose-dialogue-image}

**Authority:** Work decision — English-language adaptation adopted on 2026-08-21. Write intelligible modern English while opening necessary period military, legal, religious, diplomatic, and colonial terms through brief context. Do not simulate history with indiscriminate archaism. Vary clause, sentence, and paragraph rhythm with the pressure of marching, command, negotiation, waiting, injury, and loss while preserving the chosen English variety and each speaker's social position.

Dialogue performs action and also reveals rank, authority, fear, evasion, and relationship; it does not become a report written for the reader's convenience. Prefer images available through maps, range, weather, clothing, wounds, food, transport, handwriting, paper, buildings, tools, and terrain when those materials affect the scene. An inserted letter, decree, order, or testimony earns its space by exposing purpose or bias rather than decorating exposition.

Sources: [Cambridge Grammar on standard and nonstandard English varieties](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/uk/grammar/british-grammar/standard-and-non-standard), [Locher and Jucker on naturalism and stylization in fictional dialogue](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/fiction-and-pragmatics/D198C6EEF1402A67B259E53221B1CD16), [Purdue OWL on sentence-pattern variety and rhythm](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/sentence_variety/similar_sentence_patterns_or_rhythms.html)

## Distributed agency, record asymmetry, and cost {#historical-agency-cost}

**Authority:** Work decision — adapted from the user-supplied representation principle on 2026-08-21. Do not isolate Napoleon as a self-sufficient genius. Where the file handles collective, military, political, domestic, or colonial action, make its operation depend visibly on relevant soldiers, staff officers, officials, workers, families, allies, opponents, subjects, enslaved people, or occupied communities and on choices or constraints they actually possess. A war file carries the material costs that operate in its scope, such as conscription, supply, fatigue, disease, injury, death, displacement, confiscation, hunger, or civilian survival; it need not mechanically list costs absent from its events.

Race, slavery, colonial rule, religion, sex, and class are not erased by modern verdict alone or by adopting the period ruler's vocabulary. Read records produced by affected people alongside administrative and military records where they survive, and make archival asymmetry legible where they do not. **Success:** non-Napoleonic agency and cost alter what can happen in the file. **Failure:** token witnesses, a great-man camera that turns everyone else into scenery, or confident recovery of voices the archive did not preserve.

Sources: [Archives nationales d'outre-mer, Toussaint Louverture dossier EE 1734 and digitized family records](https://archives-nationales-outre-mer.culture.gouv.fr/decouvrir-notre-offre-culturelle/toussaint-louverture-1743-1803), [BnF Patrimoines Partagés on the 1802 Saint-Domingue expedition, plantation policy, resistance, disease, and exterminatory violence](https://heritage.bnf.fr/france-ameriques/en/1802-expedition-saint-domingue-haiti-and-louisiana-purchase), [Cambridge research on archival violence, record asymmetry, and archival silences](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/social-science-history/article/ethics-of-archives-improving-historical-social-science-through-the-consideration-of-research-on-violence/28761E79971CBC6555126DA4F6FDEEC9)

## Recurring material motifs {#recurring-material-motifs}

**Authority:** Work decision — adapted from the user-supplied motif set on 2026-08-21. Use trajectories and lines of fire, maps and distance, paper and orders, bodily fatigue, food and transport, names and forms of address only when they sharpen the current file's choice, result, or material condition. When a motif recurs within a file, vary its meaning as authority, knowledge, cost, or consequence changes. A file in which none of these motifs performs real work complies by leaving them absent, not by inserting one mechanically.

**Success:** the recurrence changes meaning with the action and helps connect private experience to institutions and material systems. **Failure:** attaching a map, order, wound, or ration as decorative period texture, or repeating a motif until it obscures the event and its agents.

Sources: [UNC Writing Center on meaning emerging through the interaction of form and concrete detail](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/literature-fiction/), [Oregon State University on setting acting upon character, conflict, and theme](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-setting)

## Prohibitions and deliberate departures {#historical-prohibitions-departures}

**Authority:** Work decision — adapted from the user-supplied prohibitions on 2026-08-21. Avoid great-man compression, foreknown inevitability, chronology substituted for narrative, moral beautification, unsupported precision, and omission of the consequential period, actor, or civilian cost owned by the file. A genre convention that would cross one of these lines may be deliberately rejected or transformed only when the text produces an observable gain in reader access and historical responsibility without violating the historical principles in `principles/common.md`.

This does not forbid admiration, horror, compression, ellipsis, composite minor characters, or contested interpretation. It requires their scope and effect to remain honest. **Failure:** using style, spectacle, or declared artistic licence to erase agency, counterfeit a source, make victory inevitable, or avoid the work's historical cost.

Sources: [Saxton on accuracy, authenticity, evidence, and constructed historical truth in fiction](https://doi.org/10.1080/13642529.2020.1727189), [Cambridge scholarship on constrained invention in postcolonial historical novels](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75), [BnF Patrimoines Partagés on the human and colonial consequences of Napoleon's Saint-Domingue policy](https://heritage.bnf.fr/france-ameriques/en/1802-expedition-saint-domingue-haiti-and-louisiana-purchase)
