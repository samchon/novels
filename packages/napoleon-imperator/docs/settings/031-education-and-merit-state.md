## Schools and state formation {#schools-and-state-formation}

**Status:** Source-supported. Schools produce officers, administrators, teachers, and citizens while filtering access through money, gender, rank, and geography.

An institution's curriculum is also a state claim about useful knowledge. A certificate may open a career without erasing class or patronage. The novel must give students teachers, schedules, buildings, examinations, and failure conditions.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon, “Les institutions de l'Instruction publique du Consulat et de l'Empire,” https://www.napoleon.org/histoire-des-2-empires/articles/les-institutions-de-linstruction-publique-du-consulat-et-de-lempire/.

## Merit and patronage {#merit-and-patronage}

**Status:** Work canon. Examinations measure performance within a system whose opportunities remain distributed by social connection.

Napoleon's career demonstrates both technical advancement and patronage. A later setting must not claim that merit alone created the imperial elite, nor that every appointment was arbitrary. Letters, vacancies, recommendations, and performance all matter.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, “The correspondence of Napoleon,” https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/.

## Education of women and family law {#education-of-women-and-family-law}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Female education, marriage, inheritance, and household authority differ from male military and administrative routes.

The imperial state educates women partly for domestic and dynastic roles while limiting legal independence. The settings graph must not measure Josephine, Letizia, Marie-Louise, or female subjects against a male career path.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Boundary to institutional reform {#boundary-to-institutional-reform}

**Status:** Work boundary. Lycées, École polytechnique, military schools, and local primary education require distinct owners and dates.

Educational reform affects recruitment and administration gradually. A new decree does not immediately create trained teachers, buildings, books, or equal access.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/.
