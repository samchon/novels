## Scientists and state patronage {#scientists-and-state-patronage}

**Status:** Source-supported. The state funds useful knowledge while directing it toward military, administrative, colonial, and prestige goals.

Scientists need instruments, assistants, publications, travel permits, and protection. A discovery becomes state capacity only through workshops, budgets, standards, and trained users. The novel must preserve uncertainty and failed experiments, and must distinguish a researcher's observation from the ministry's later claim about its use.

Sources: Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Roads canals ports and bridges {#roads-canals-ports-and-bridges}

**Status:** Work canon. Infrastructure changes military speed, trade, taxation, and civilian life but requires labor, land, money, and maintenance.

A road on a map may be muddy, privately controlled, destroyed, or too narrow for artillery. Bridges can be strategic objects and local burdens. Every campaign setting must inherit actual route capacity rather than using distance alone, and must identify who repairs, guards, taxes, or closes the route.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, “Napoleon's Grande Armée” (1), https://www.napoleon.org/en/young-historians/napodoc/napoleons-grande-armee-1/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Industrial production and war supply {#industrial-production-and-war-supply}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Weapons, uniforms, powder, shoes, paper, and wagons come from factories, workshops, contracts, and coerced or paid labor.

Mass armies expose bottlenecks. A decree can order production but cannot instantly create skilled workers or raw materials. Shortages must appear in depots and bodies, not only in an abstract economy. Settings must name the workshop, raw material, transport, inspection, and user who turns an order into a serviceable object.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Standards measurement and repair {#standards-measurement-and-repair}

**Status:** Work canon. Technical systems depend on measurement, interchangeable practice, inspection, maintenance, and repair workers.

A gun, bridge, uniform, map, or medical instrument fails when a part is missing, a measurement differs, or the user lacks training. Later scenes must identify the standard, tool, worker, and test that make equipment reliable. “Modern” is not a magical property; it is repeated labor under institutional constraints.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.

## Infrastructure and civilian displacement {#infrastructure-and-civilian-displacement}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Roads, canals, ports, and fortifications impose land, labor, requisition, and security costs on people who do not command them.

A military road can improve trade while displacing tenants; a bridge can connect markets while exposing a village to every passing army. Settings must identify landowners, laborers, tolls, guards, and the season in which the infrastructure is usable. Civilian benefit and military coercion can coexist without being the same experience.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Industrial limits and campaign logistics {#industrial-limits-and-campaign-logistics}

**Status:** Source-critical boundary. Production capacity is bounded by coal, timber, metal, cloth, skilled labor, transport, money, and the distance from depot to army.

A factory output figure does not prove that the front receives usable supplies. Later campaign settings must connect contracts to inspection, packing, wagons, bridges, weather, and receipt. A shortage can arise from production, allocation, theft, damage, or delay; these causes must not be silently combined.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Boundary to scientific work in Egypt {#boundary-to-scientific-work-in-egypt}

**Status:** Work boundary. The Egyptian expedition's scholars and colonial knowledge require the localized owner in `009-egypt-and-eastern-expedition.md` and its descendants.

Imperial science can preserve evidence while participating in occupation. Later settings must identify local knowledge, ownership, translation, and extraction, and must not treat publication as proof that the people who supplied knowledge received its benefits or consented to its use.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Boundary to technical storylines {#boundary-to-technical-storylines}

**Status:** Work boundary. Specific engineers, workshops, inventions, and industrial regions require their own historical and narrative owners.

Every later scene must identify the object, maker, standard, transport, and user whose labor it depends on. The settings layer hands forward capacity and limits, not a generalized promise of progress.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.
