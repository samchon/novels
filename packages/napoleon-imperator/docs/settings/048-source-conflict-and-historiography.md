# Source Conflict and Historiography

This file owns the package's method for admitting, comparing, and limiting historical claims.

## Source identity and provenance {#source-identity-and-provenance}
<!-- @evidence obligations/common.md#scope-preservation This H2 owns author, institution, document type, date, audience, edition, archive, collection, claim, and locator. -->
<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#scope-preservation #b3e71a4 Compared provenance scope with 037, 038, 039, 040, 041, and 047; source identity remains distinct from later interpretation. -->
<!-- @evidence obligations/common.md#substantive-completion This H2 settles the identifying fields and supported claim needed before a source can carry historical precision. -->
<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#substantive-completion #c6a92d8 Tested author, institution, type, date, audience, edition, archive, claim, locator, and use. -->
<!-- @evidence obligations/common.md#proportionate-development This H2 separates primary document, memoir, official collection, critical study, archive, editor, and downstream claimant. -->
<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#proportionate-development #e4b17f2 Checked each source role for access, authority, date, and limitation. -->
<!-- @evidence obligations/common.md#evidence-content-conformance This host refuses URL-only proof and requires the source identity and claim scope to be recoverable. -->
<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#evidence-content-conformance #a7d83c1 Rechecked correspondence metadata and archive/article identity before retaining the provenance rule. -->

**Status:** Work canon. Every external claim enters through an identifiable document, edition, archive, collection, or critical study.

A URL alone is not an argument. Settings must name author or institution, document type, date, audience, edition, and the claim supported. A later summary cannot silently replace a primary record when precision matters.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

Direct locators: Fondation Napoléon’s correspondence resource identifies the collection's dated corpus, editorial presentation, and archive/index structure (lines 80-85 and 109-121), https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/.

**Status:** Source-critical. Conflicting accounts are preserved, compared, and bounded rather than averaged into false certainty.

Official bulletins, memoirs, enemy reports, letters, and later scholarship have different positions and incentives. The owning H2 must say what agrees, what conflicts, and what the novel selects for a bounded reason. A clean compiler does not settle a historical dispute.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon correspondence, https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Precision and unsupported detail {#precision-and-unsupported-detail}

**Status:** Work canon. Dates, counts, quotations, locations, motives, and sensory details receive only the precision the source supports.

An approximate march cannot become an exact minute sequence; an attributed memory cannot become verbatim dialogue; a disputed casualty count cannot become a certain total. Settings must mark approximate, contested, inferred, selected, or unknown status before downstream use.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

Direct locators: the Austerlitz study places orders, observers, and command knowledge within a battle narrative rather than granting all actors the same view (lines 68-85 and 100-131); the correspondence resource identifies dated documents and editorial framing (lines 80-85 and 109-121).

<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#scope-preservation #b3e71a4 Compared reconstruction scope with 037, 038, 039, 040, 041, 044, and 047; invention remains bounded and legible. -->

<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#proportionate-development #e4b17f2 Checked each narrative function for a source limit and non-archival status. -->

<!-- @evidenceReview obligations/common.md#evidence-content-conformance #a7d83c1 Rechecked shared principles and package source rules before retaining the reconstruction boundary. -->

**Status:** Work boundary. Fiction may connect supported facts with bounded invention only when the invention does not contradict source, knowledge, chronology, material possibility, or ethical representation.

Invented dialogue, unnamed composite labor, exact private feeling, and unrecorded minute sequences are risks. Later layers must label the narrative function through structure and focalization without pretending that invented material is archival fact. Silence is preferable to fabricated certainty when the distinction matters.

**Status:** Work boundary. The disabled stage records source standings in prose; evidence and review stages activate graph diagnostics and current fingerprints.

No tag can repair a false claim, missing source, duplicate unit, or unresolved contradiction. When the layer advances, each H2 must be reread, its claim-source relation tested, and any repair propagated to downstream descendants.
