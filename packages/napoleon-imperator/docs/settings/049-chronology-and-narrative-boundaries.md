**Status:** Work canon. Napoleon's life is a sequence of dated places, offices, relationships, bodies, and institutions whose states change over time.

A date alone does not establish presence, knowledge, authority, or action. Settings must identify place, source, state, and what changed. Later layers may connect events only when the relevant people and resources can physically meet.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology portal, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Fondation Napoléon early years, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/timelines/1769-1793-napoleon-bonapartes-early-years/.

Disabled completion is not evidence or review completion. The next stage must activate diagnostics, repair real content defects, fingerprint reread acknowledgements, and pass an independent full-layer gate before storylines begin.
