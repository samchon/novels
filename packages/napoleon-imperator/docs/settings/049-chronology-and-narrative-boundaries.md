**Status:** Work canon. Napoleon's life is a sequence of dated places, offices, relationships, bodies, and institutions whose states change over time.

A date alone does not establish presence, knowledge, authority, or action. Settings must identify place, source, state, and what changed. Later layers may connect events only when the relevant people and resources can physically meet.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology portal, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Fondation Napoléon early years, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/timelines/1769-1793-napoleon-bonapartes-early-years/.

**Status:** Source-critical. Revolutionary, Gregorian, local, astronomical, and retrospective dates can describe different temporal systems.

Every exact date must retain its calendar and source. Approximate periods must not be converted into false precision. A letter date, event date, publication date, and receipt date are separate fields with separate evidence.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon correspondence, https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

**Status:** Work canon. No focal figure receives later memoir knowledge, campaign outcome, or archival explanation before the relevant information arrives.

The reader may know an outcome while a character sees only an order, rumor, body, map, or silence. Later narratives must state observer, channel, delay, and uncertainty. A memoir remains a later document with its own bias.

**Status:** Work boundary. Settings owns facts and constraints; storylines own connections and event accumulation; scenarios own executable scene mechanics; manuscripts own finished prose.

If a downstream document exposes a false fact or missing condition, the earliest owning setting changes first, descendants are reread, and reviews renew. No narrative layer may hide a contradiction by preserving an attractive but unsupported upstream decision.

Disabled completion is not evidence or review completion. The next stage must activate diagnostics, repair real content defects, fingerprint reread acknowledgements, and pass an independent full-layer gate before storylines begin.
