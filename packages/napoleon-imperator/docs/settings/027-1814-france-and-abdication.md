Local defense, fortresses, roads, Parisian politics, and army movement are coupled. A battlefield victory can fail to restore the national position if the capital, allies, or supplies are lost. The novel must show scale and scarcity together: a small force may move quickly while a larger one is tied to depots, wounded, guns, and political instructions.
