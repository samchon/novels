## Defending France with a shrinking state {#defending-france-with-a-shrinking-state}

**Status:** Source-supported. Napoleon's military skill operates amid exhausted resources, coalition invasion, conscription fatigue, and threatened administration.

Local defense, fortresses, roads, Parisian politics, and army movement are coupled. A battlefield victory can fail to restore the national position if the capital, allies, or supplies are lost. The novel must show scale and scarcity together.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Diplomacy at the edge of Paris {#diplomacy-at-the-edge-of-paris}

**Status:** Work canon. Negotiation, military movement, public opinion, and the Senate interact; no single chamber speaks for France.

Officials receive contradictory reports and weigh survival, legitimacy, property, and foreign terms. The emperor's orders may be militarily sound but politically impossible. Scenes must identify the audience and coercive power behind every declaration.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon, correspondence overview, https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/.

## Abdication as document and rupture {#abdication-as-document-and-rupture}

**Status:** Source-supported. Abdication ends one legal claim while leaving family, army, property, and succession disputes unresolved.

The act's wording, date, audience, and constitutional interpretation matter. It is not the same as battlefield surrender or exile. The settings graph must connect it to the provisional government and allied occupation.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Boundary to Elba {#boundary-to-elba}

**Status:** Work boundary. Treaty arrangements, travel, guards, and island administration belong to the Elba owner.

Abdication creates the legal and political condition for exile but does not determine the journey's exact escort, communications, or local reception. Those facts must be sourced separately.

Sources: Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.
