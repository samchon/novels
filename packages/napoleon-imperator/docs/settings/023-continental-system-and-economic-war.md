This file owns the blockade as an administrative, commercial, and coercive system.

## Blockade decree and enforcement gap {#blockade-decree-and-enforcement-gap}

**Status:** Source-supported. A decree prohibiting British commerce requires customs officials, ports, patrols, courts, informers, and cooperation from allies and occupied states.

The Continental System is not a wall. Smuggling, neutral shipping, forged papers, corrupt officials, local scarcity, and diplomatic exceptions create gaps. A central order can be obeyed publicly and evaded privately. The novel must show who profits and who pays.

Sources: Gallica, Napoleonic legal collections, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Ports merchants and contraband {#ports-merchants-and-contraband}

**Status:** Work canon. Economic war is lived in warehouses, customs houses, ships, credit networks, and household prices.

A merchant facing confiscation may bribe an inspector, reroute cargo, appeal to a consul, or stop trading. A port town experiences unemployment and scarcity alongside official patriotism. Scenes must track paperwork, currency, ships, and the physical risk of concealment.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Allied compliance and political resentment {#allied-compliance-and-political-resentment}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Allies enforce French economic policy unevenly because their own states, merchants, and consumers bear the cost.

Coercion can produce compliance and make an ally less reliable. The policy affects Russia, Spain, the German states, Italy, and the Netherlands differently. A diplomatic dispute over commerce can become a military crisis without a single dramatic insult.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.

## Boundary to 1812 rupture {#boundary-to-1812-rupture}

**Status:** Work boundary. The Franco-Russian breakdown requires its own diplomatic and campaign setting; this file supplies economic pressure and enforcement mechanics.

The system's failure is cumulative: lost revenue, allied resentment, smuggling, naval limits, and divergent strategic aims. No later scene may state that one decree alone caused the Russian war.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/.
