An imperial budget is not cash in a single vault. Revenue arrives at different times and places, while armies need immediate pay, food, horses, and ammunition. A delayed tax payment can become a military shortage or a local revolt. Settings must identify assessment, collection, transport, accounting, and enforcement rather than treating tax as an abstract flow.
