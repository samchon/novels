# Finance Tax and Debt

This file owns the fiscal systems that make war and administration possible.

## Revenue and collection {#revenue-and-collection}

**Status:** Source-supported work canon. Taxes require registers, collectors, courts, local cooperation, transport, and the ability to enforce payment.

An imperial budget is not cash in a single vault. Revenue arrives at different times and places, while armies need immediate pay, food, horses, and ammunition. A delayed tax payment can become a military shortage or a local revolt.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## War indemnities and occupied economies {#war-indemnities-and-occupied-economies}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Treaty payments, requisitions, confiscation, and occupation costs redistribute wealth and create resentment.

An order for supplies becomes carts seized from a town, coin removed from a treasury, or a merchant unpaid. The novel must identify beneficiaries, intermediaries, and those deprived, not describe extraction as weightless victory.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Bank, credit, and confidence {#bank-credit-and-confidence}

**Status:** Work canon. State borrowing and commercial credit depend on confidence, law, collateral, and the expectation of future revenue.

A government can announce solvency while merchants refuse paper or demand security. War news changes prices and credit before an official report arrives. Financial scenes must specify document, currency, institution, and risk.

Sources: Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Boundary to household debt {#boundary-to-household-debt}

**Status:** Work boundary. Individual creditors, estates, dowries, and Bonaparte family finance require household-level owners.

State finance and private debt intersect but are not identical. A minister can be solvent while a soldier's family starves; an imperial grant can create a new dependency. Later settings must preserve that scale difference.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.
