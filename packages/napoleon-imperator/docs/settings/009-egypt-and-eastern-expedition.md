**Status:** Source-supported for the expedition's military, commercial, and imperial objectives; exact private motives remain contested.

Egypt offers a route toward British India, a base in the eastern Mediterranean, and an arena for scientific and cultural claims. The BnF describes the expedition as both a conquest intended to block Britain's route to India and a scientific expedition; the Institute account adds commercial/political aims and a Commission of 167 academics. The 19 May 1798 embarkation and the Institute object's note that the destination was withheld from scientists until at sea make secrecy a dated, uneven condition. Ship movement, provisioning, officers, foreign observers, and orders to a large force still leak information. A plan can be secret at Paris while obvious to a port worker or enemy scout.
