The law of 28 Pluviôse an VIII (17 February 1800) reorganized departments around a prefect, secretary general, council of prefecture, and council general. The institutional study identifies the prefect as appointed and removable by the head of state, “charged alone with the administration,” able to direct services, police, spending, taxation, and levies while corresponding with ministers. A central decision is effective only when a local official has staff, money, records, coercive capacity, and roads. The novel must show both upward filtering and downward delay.
