**Status:** Work canon. Terrain, season, light, mist, rain, smoke, and roads determine what units can see, hear, carry, and reach.

A map does not provide a witness's horizon. Settings must identify slope, surface, water, buildings, vegetation, weather, and the observer's position before assigning movement or knowledge. Visibility can be limited without limiting sound, and sound can be confused without being absent.

A casualty count depends on category, time, source, and whether missing men are later found. Settings must identify who collects wounded, which route remains open, who guards prisoners, and what report counts. The field does not end when firing stops.
