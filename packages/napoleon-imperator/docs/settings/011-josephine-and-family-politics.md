**Status:** Work boundary grounded in imperial dynastic history. Divorce, Marie-Louise, the king of Rome, and later family consequences require dedicated owners.

The genealogy records the marriage as dissolved in 1809 and declared null in 1810; the Eugène biography places Eugène in France during the divorce process from December 1809 to February 1810. The later succession owner must handle the Senate declaration, ceremony, Austrian diplomacy, and Marie-Louise separately, but this file preserves the cost to Josephine and the children. Fertility, law, ceremony, diplomacy, and personal loss affect the Beauharnais and Bonaparte branches, the court, and public legitimacy. A dynastic decision is not a simple private replacement, and it does not prove that earlier affection was unreal.
