# Ministers and Administrators

This file owns the civil offices that translate imperial decisions into law, money, policing, and daily administration.

<!-- principles/common.md#purpose-fit principles/common.md#layer-boundary principles/common.md#work-language -->

## Ministerial office and competing jurisdictions {#ministerial-office-and-competing-jurisdictions}

**Status:** Work canon. A ministry has a legal remit, budget, staff, archives, and rivals; it is not identical with the emperor's intention.

Orders cross ministries, prefectures, courts, police, army staffs, and households. Settings must identify which office can decide, which can execute, and which can delay or report. A decree may be clear in Paris and ambiguous at a frontier.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.

## Prefects and local implementation {#prefects-and-local-implementation}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Prefects connect central policy to municipalities, taxation, conscription, policing, roads, and public information.

A prefect cannot make a decree true without clerks, registers, guards, money, and local cooperation. Reports travel upward through incentives and fear. Later scenes must distinguish the central instruction, the prefect's interpretation, the mayor's action, and the population's response.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Police surveillance and security {#police-surveillance-and-security}

**Status:** Work canon. Security is produced through informers, files, patrols, censorship, arrests, prisons, and judicial or extrajudicial authority.

An intelligence report is not an event and an arrest is not proof of guilt. Settings must identify the source, chain of custody, legal authority, physical site, and consequence of a report. Police may know a rumor without knowing its truth, while fear changes what citizens say publicly.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Law courts and administrative continuity {#law-courts-and-administrative-continuity}

**Status:** Source-critical. Regime change can preserve offices and procedures while changing whose rights, property, and speech the system protects.

Courts require files, judges, clerks, witnesses, police, and time. A legal code is implemented unevenly, and an imperial command may conflict with local custom or judicial procedure. Later documents must identify the jurisdiction, text, date, and enforcement path before calling an act legal or illegal.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Ministries and information bottlenecks {#ministries-and-information-bottlenecks}

**Status:** Work canon. Files accumulate, wait, move, and lose context; administrative power is limited by time and attention.

A minister receives summaries rather than every fact. A missing enclosure, untranslated letter, or delayed register can change policy. Settings must show who prepares a brief, what evidence is omitted, who signs, and how a subordinate learns of the decision.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon correspondence, https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Administrative coercion and ordinary life {#administrative-coercion-and-ordinary-life}

**Status:** Source-required domain. The state reaches households through identity papers, taxes, conscription, licenses, schooling, travel controls, and requisition.

An ordinary person meets an institution at a desk, checkpoint, court, market, barracks, or parish. Settings must identify the document, fee, official, queue, and remedy. Efficiency and coercion can coexist; neither should be described without the person who performs and bears it.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.

## Boundary to ministries and character owners {#boundary-to-ministries-and-character-owners}

**Status:** Work boundary. Individual ministers, prefects, judges, police officials, and clerks require dated biographical and institutional owners.

This file supplies administrative mechanics and evidence limits. Downstream units must cite the office, document, official, jurisdiction, and material consequence they use; they may not turn the state into a single voice or a frictionless machine.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Fondation Napoléon correspondence, https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/.
