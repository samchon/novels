**Status:** Source-critical. Historical medicine contains observation, theory, practice, hierarchy, and uncertainty that must not be rewritten as modern diagnosis.

A doctor records symptoms through contemporary categories and limited instruments. Settings must distinguish observation, treatment, prognosis, and later interpretation. The manuscript may render bodily experience without asserting a disputed cause as settled fact.
