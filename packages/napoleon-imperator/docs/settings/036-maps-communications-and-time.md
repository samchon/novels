**Status:** Work canon. Maps select roads, heights, settlements, and boundaries; they do not replace reconnaissance or local knowledge.

A commander reads a map through scale, symbols, survey date, and purpose. A village may have moved, a road may be seasonal, and a contour may hide a ravine. Scenarios must distinguish map knowledge from sight and report, and must identify whose map is available at the moment of action.
