## Peninsula geography and divided authority {#peninsula-geography-and-divided-authority}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Mountains, roads, cities, ports, languages, local jurisdictions, and seasonal weather frustrate centralized command.

An army can occupy a capital while failing to control its roads or countryside. French, Spanish, Portuguese, British, local, and guerrilla forces receive different information and use different supply systems. The settings graph must not reduce Iberia to a sequence of battles.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Peninsula roads and fragmented sovereignty {#peninsula-roads-and-fragmented-sovereignty}

**Status:** Source-required domain. Mountains, roads, ports, languages, municipal jurisdictions, and seasons prevent a single occupation map from describing Iberia.

An army can hold a capital while losing its roads, couriers, and countryside. French, Spanish, Portuguese, British, local, and guerrilla forces possess different information and supply. Every operation must identify which authority controls a bridge, town, tax office, and road at that moment. Distance is operational rather than merely cartographic: a road that carries a column in dry weather may become a bottleneck after rain, while a port may remain open to one navy and closed to another. Settings must distinguish a garrison's nominal jurisdiction from the radius it can patrol, feed, and communicate with before darkness.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/.

## Bayonne dynastic intervention and legitimacy {#bayonne-dynastic-intervention-and-legitimacy}

**Status:** Work canon. Dynastic replacement affects clergy, municipalities, property, military allegiance, and family identity; a decree cannot manufacture acceptance.

Ceremony and coercion operate together. A new king requires proclamations, officers, revenue, guards, interpreters, and local officials. Residents may comply publicly, resist privately, or use the new court for their own interests. The novel must not treat Spanish actors as a single reaction. A setting may show a council receiving an order, but it must separately establish whether the order is copied, translated, obeyed, delayed, or quietly evaded. The legitimacy contest belongs to institutions and households as much as to royal portraits or battlefield declarations.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Insurgency intelligence and reprisal {#insurgency-intelligence-and-reprisal}

**Status:** Source-critical. French reports often describe resistance as brigandage; local fighters, civilians, clergy, informers, and regular troops overlap.

Control depends on guides, hostages, patrols, reprisals, food, and protection. A road cleared at noon can be unsafe by evening. The story must show who provides information, how it is tested, and what a mistaken classification costs civilians. Reports should preserve the observer's position and incentive: a frightened courier, a village priest, a French officer seeking reinforcement, and a partisan commander do not see the same event. Reprisal is a material choice involving prisoners, transport, paperwork, and command responsibility, not an abstract mood. Later scenes may dramatize consequences only when the setting has identified the chain that authorizes violence.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## British Portuguese endurance and supply {#british-portuguese-endurance-and-supply}

**Status:** Work boundary. Allied sea supply, local cooperation, retreat discipline, and Wellington's operations require campaign-specific owners.

An opponent with secure ports, protected magazines, local allies, and a disciplined retreat can deny the French decisive battle. The peninsula war is therefore a long contest of supply and legitimacy, not a series of French battlefield failures alone. Later settings must give allied commanders agency. They must also distinguish British pay and shipping from Portuguese levies, local requisition, and civilian survival: an allied army's endurance depends on contracts, roads, animals, engineers, and political consent. A retreat can be an organized preservation of force while still imposing hunger, displacement, and destroyed stores on the population.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Bayonne institutions and dynastic intervention {#bayonne-institutions-and-dynastic-intervention}

**Status:** Source-supported for Napoleon's intervention in Spanish dynastic politics; exact legal and personal consequences require a dedicated owner.

Dynastic replacement affects legitimacy, clergy, municipal authority, military allegiance, and family identity. A decree from the emperor cannot make a foreign king accepted. The novel must show ceremony and coercion together, including the officials who copy, translate, enforce, delay, or quietly evade a new order.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/.

## Insurgency and counterinsurgency {#insurgency-and-counterinsurgency}

**Status:** Work canon. Local fighters, civilians, clergy, bandits, regular troops, and informers overlap; French reports may collapse them into brigands.

Control depends on intelligence, hostages, reprisals, escorts, food, and protection. A road cleared in the morning can be unsafe at dusk. Civilian harm is not an incidental cost but part of the occupation mechanism. Any later scene must identify the observer, the information test, the authority authorizing force, and the material consequence of a mistaken classification.

Sources: Archives nationales, https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Wellington and allied endurance {#wellington-and-allied-endurance}

**Status:** Source-supported for British-Portuguese cooperation as a major strategic constraint; individual campaigns require separate owners.

An opponent with secure sea supply, local allies, disciplined retreat, and intelligence can deny a decisive French battle. The novel must give allied commanders strategic agency and avoid treating French occupation reports as the whole war. British pay and shipping, Portuguese levies, local requisition, roads, animals, engineers, and political consent all shape endurance. A retreat may preserve an army while imposing hunger, displacement, and destroyed stores on civilians.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology portal, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.
