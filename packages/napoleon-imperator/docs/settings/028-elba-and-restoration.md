# Elba and Restoration

This file owns Napoleon's first exile and return from Elba.

## Island sovereignty and restricted empire {#island-sovereignty-and-restricted-empire}

**Status:** Source-supported. Elba offers Napoleon a constrained title, territory, household, revenue problem, and diplomatic vulnerability.

An island government needs money, staff, police, roads, ports, and local cooperation. The former emperor's title does not guarantee supplies or recognition. The setting must distinguish ceremonial authority from effective control.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/; Gallica, https://gallica.bnf.fr/html/und/histoire/napoleon-bonaparte.

## Surveillance and information from exile {#surveillance-and-information-from-exile}

**Status:** Work canon. Letters, visitors, ships, newspapers, agents, and British or allied reports shape what Napoleon knows about France.

Information is delayed and curated. A visitor may carry rumour; a newspaper may reflect a faction; an official report may monitor the island. Decisions to leave depend on uncertain estimates of army loyalty and Bourbon weakness.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, “The correspondence of Napoleon,” https://fondationnapoleon.org/en/activities-and-services/telling-history/correspondence-napoleon/.

## Return voyage and landing network {#return-voyage-and-landing-network}

**Status:** Source-required domain. The return requires ships, officers, proclamations, routes, local contacts, and the calculation that troops may join rather than arrest Napoleon.

The landing is a chain of risks, not a miraculous national welcome. Each town and regiment receives different information. A proclamation can open a door while a nearby official closes it. Scenarios must preserve the uncertainty until contact occurs.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon chronology, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/chronologies/.

## Boundary to the Hundred Days {#boundary-to-the-hundred-days}

**Status:** Work boundary. The political reconstruction, Waterloo campaign, and second abdication belong to later owners, with `021-waterloo-and-saint-helena.md` holding the terminal arc.

Elba ends when Napoleon reaches the French mainland and the state's response changes. The next file must inherit uncertain legitimacy, returning veterans, family strategy, and coalition mobilization.

Sources: Fondation Napoléon, biography, https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/biographies/napoleon-bonaparte/.
