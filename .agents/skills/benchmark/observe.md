# Observe and Intervene

At the user-requested cadence, inspect filesystem output and compiler state rather than trusting self-reports. Send concise progress updates during long runs and a substantive critique at the agreed interval.

## Stage-Calibrated Supervision

Judge an artifact at its active evidence state. Do not impose an `evidence` or `review` completion gate on a layer that is still producing its complete first version in `disabled`.

While a layer is `disabled`, let its owner finish the complete coherent first version with real creative authority. Observe samples, builds, scope, and direction, and record suspected defects for the evidence pass, but do not interrupt, delete a batch, require per-file or per-unit approval, or demand complete citations, evidence coverage, exclusions, or review fingerprints merely because an unfinished draft is rough, provisional, disputed, or incomplete. A partial sample does not certify or condemn the whole layer.

Intervene during `disabled` only for failures independent of evidence maturity: an ignored nonzero package build, an unauthorized shared or foreign-package edit, concurrent writers in one package, a stage-order violation, destructive corruption, deliberate harness weakening, or downstream authorship begun before the upstream layer is complete. Preserve sound work when correcting such a failure.

When the owner judges that the complete first version satisfies the [staging transition prerequisites](../evidence-graph/staging.md#transitions), the owner advances the whole layer to `evidence` and runs the package build to collect the real diagnostic population. The coordinator neither grants prior permission nor substitutes its judgment for that transition. That first diagnostic set is input to the evidence pass, not proof that the owner failed. Let the owner resolve it across the complete layer: treat each diagnostic as a question about the artifact, repair canon, hierarchy, substance, or lineage when those are deficient, and only then write the truthful acknowledgement or concrete exclusion that remains. An intermediate failing build is expected while the owner is actively repairing that diagnostic population; proceeding as though it passed, weakening the harness, or replacing repair with false tags is not.

## Evidence-Stage Supervision

As soon as the configured layer actually enters `evidence`, increase semantic supervision without taking authorship away from its owner. Inspect representative diagnostics and every newly committed evidence pattern against the complete host, exact target, selected descendants, claim population, active config, and applicable principle or obligation. Check that file-level principles remain file-level, unit obligations and lineage sit on their exact H2/H3/H4 hosts, exclusions are population-wide and concrete, and the package config activates the intended target population at the same stage as its hosts.

Intervene immediately when an owner writes a false or exaggerated acknowledgement, copies generic reasons, paraphrases the target name instead of proving the relationship, uses blanket or conflicting exclusions, places evidence at a broader host to hide a weak child, invents a review or fingerprint, weakens a claim or state, ignores a nonzero build, or treats a content defect as evidence work to postpone again. Evidence is not permission to preserve a false premise. When truthful acknowledgement exposes missing canon, bundled ownership, broken causality, shallow execution, or prose that cannot support the claim, require the earliest owning content and every affected dependant to be repaired before the tag remains.

If a diagnostic or evidence audit shows that local tag edits would merely conceal a systemic defect, interrupt the owner, confirm writes have stopped, invalidate the affected coherent scope, and require reconstruction from a correct outline. A widespread false-evidence batch may be removed while stopped or reverted to the last clean checkpoint before the owner resumes; preserve unrelated sound work. Replace the owner only when the observed pattern and run authority justify it. Do not confuse an intermediate failing build during active truthful repair with evasion, but never allow unrelated downstream progress, review, or a reported clean gate while it fails.

The strong full-layer intervention culminates after the owner resolves the evidence diagnostics and obtains a clean evidence build. Literally audit the complete layer then. Compiler-green boilerplate, decorative citations, false acknowledgements, incoherent canon, inert settings, skeletal treatment, unstaged scenarios, or weak prose still fails. `review` adds independent rereading and current fingerprints to that already substantive, evidence-clean artifact; it is not the first literary-quality pass.

Audit samples across every active work:

- settings domain coverage, independent H2 owners, paragraph structure, factual sourcing, unresolved canon, arithmetic, and downstream usability;
- storyline reader flow, semantic H2/H3/H4 boundaries, paragraph structure, chosen connection, cumulative development, applicable agency, and concrete treatment; scenario executability, action and dialogue blocks, physical or formal progression, applicable pivotal dialogue or silence, and spatial control; manuscript access, voice, literary or formal effect, rhythm, and applicable emotional consequence;
- H4 continuity of time, place, focal mode, function, entry, central progression, and exit;
- truthful lineage, concrete evidence and exclusion reasons, substantive review text, and current fingerprints;
- truthful unit-by-unit answers to `obligations/common.md`, including scope preservation against the brief and proportional development against the historical baseline;
- package-local claim target classification and naming, truthful populations and cardinality, and the absence of empty contract families;
- preservation of user-confirmed work rules, the distinction between those rules and owner decisions, observable realization of the selected work-specific style or form, and stage-aligned package claims;
- stage direction, package-only ownership, placeholders, probes, status files, diagnostic-silencing edits, or other repository pollution.

At the stage-specific gates above, intervene when independent settings hide under an umbrella H2, an H4 is merely a large partition, settings or treatment hides multiple functions in a prose slab, a storyline is an event list or causal card, setting detail is inert or still leaves downstream guessing, a scenario hides mechanics in summary or templated beats, a script buries dialogue in prose or omits a required exchange or operative turn, prose expands field labels, citations are decorative, exclusions use generic layer excuses, reviews say only "checked," or a compiler-green work is dull or incoherent. Do not micromanage sound creative choices, sanitize difficult subject matter, or rank agents by raw counts.

Also intervene when a direct directive survives only in the brief, an owner silently changes its authority, a vague style label substitutes for observable choices, a work-specific role is misfiled as settings canon or a checklist, or a package target exists without an active stage-aligned claim.

When an active owner triggers an intervention, interrupt it first and confirm its work has stopped. Inspect the artifact and compiler state, send the observed evidence and required repair while it is stopped, then explicitly resume it with that repair task. This applies especially to stage-order violations, false common-obligation acknowledgements or reviews, ignored build failures, diagnostic-silencing edits, and downstream work begun over an incomplete upstream layer. Route every evidence diagnostic through `$evidence-graph` [Diagnostics](../evidence-graph/SKILL.md#diagnostics). A message left only in a running agent's queue is not an intervention.

Fix a genuine shared defect once in its canonical config or skill, verify it, and require every affected owner to recheck. Never weaken the harness for one lagging work.
