# Settings Principles

Criteria for settings files that establish the work's facts and constraints.

## Addressable canon {#addressable-canon}

Give every independent canon element owned by a file one stable canonical H2. Do not bundle characters, relationships, groups, institutions, places, events, objects, resources, rules, customs, capabilities, limits, or source standings that can be cited, changed, refuted, or reviewed separately. Even one major entity receives separate H2 units for aspects such as status, authority, capability, relationships, or factual standing when those aspects have independent consumers.

Keep under an H2 only dependent attributes inseparable from that owner. Do not hide independent elements in overview prose before the first H2, H3/H4 headings, lists, tables, group headings, or another element's description. Refer to an element owned by another file instead of redefining it. A file classifies related H2 units; it does not replace their evidence addresses.

This item asks only about the address structure of settings files. The settings canon-coverage-map obligation governs which domains are needed, the common scope-preservation obligation governs missing content, and substantive completion governs the depth of each H2.

Sources: [NASA on unique identification and one requirement per statement](https://swehb.nasa.gov/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=146540037), [Marie-Laure Ryan on storyworld existents and events holding states in spacetime](https://marilaur.info/degrees.pdf), [ttsc on coverage at individual host and reference units](https://ttsc.dev/docs/evidence/claims/)

## Information structure {#information-structure}

After fact status, each H2 begins with a brief orienting paragraph that states the identity of its owner, the central decision, its applicable boundary, and downstream significance. Following paragraphs develop definition, conditions, support, relationships, exceptions, and consequences by function. The orientation does not duplicate the detailed body; lists and tables supplement prose only when they clarify comparison or correspondence.

Give each paragraph one central claim or one inseparable claim bundle. Separate distinct conditions, bodies of support, exceptions, or consequences; rejoin fragments that do not perform an independent function. Do not judge by fixed character or paragraph counts.

This item asks about the arrangement and reviewability of information inside an H2. Addressable canon governs separation into H2 owners, and the common substantive-completion obligation governs sufficient depth.

Sources: [Purdue OWL on paragraph focus and support](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/graduate_writing/introduction_to_writing/documents/drafting-your-document/organization-at-the-paragraph-level.pdf), [George Mason University on reverse outlining paragraph functions and order](https://writingcenter.gmu.edu/writing-resources/writing-as-process/reverse-outlining)

## Fact status {#fact-status}

Distinguish facts verified by external evidence from facts chosen for the work, matters under dispute, and matters not yet settled. Do not present an inference or provisional decision as settled fact.

Mark an unsettled matter as unresolved and record its decision condition. This item governs factual standing only; source support governs source quality.

Sources: [Cambridge scholarship on the relation between recorded fact and invention](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75), [Jane Friedman on distinguishing fact and fiction in historical novels](https://janefriedman.com/dos-and-donts-of-writing-historical-fiction/), [Laterpress on the series bible as a continuity record](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/)

## Source support {#source-support}

For externally verifiable claims in history, law, medicine, science, culture, and comparable fields, prefer primary evidence, official records, scholarship, and critical editions. Record the source's date, bias, scope, and materially different conclusions.

Identify the document, record item, edition, volume, page, section, date, or direct page that actually supports each claim so a reader can retrieve it. A search result, archive entrance, collection description, or whole correspondence catalogue is a discovery path rather than evidence unless that page itself states the claim.

Do not invent precision the evidence does not support. Do not attach fabricated external authority to a fact chosen for the work; mark it as a decision. When direct support cannot be found, return the claim to unresolved status or reduce its precision rather than attaching a broad portal and recording review as complete.

Sources: [the US National Archives on identifying records down to item, file unit, series, record group, and repository](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/citation.html), [W3C PROV-O on distinguishing primary sources and derivation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), [Cambridge scholarship on the relation between recorded fact and invention](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75)

## Constraint sufficiency {#constraint-sufficiency}

State the limits, costs, exceptions, authority, resources, and dependencies of an adopted rule or constraint so downstream layers do not invent a core condition. Judge sufficiency by whether later choices can be evaluated, not by word count.

When a value cannot yet be fixed, record the permitted range or decision condition. Do not conceal a blank and leave interpretation to downstream work.

This item asks only whether adopted rules and constraints have usable decision boundaries. The common substantive-completion obligation governs whether the H2 is a complete settings deliverable, and minimal departure governs which real-world defaults need explicit recording.

Sources: [Ronen on the necessary indeterminacy of fictional worlds](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/possible-worlds-in-literary-theory/fictional-entities-incomplete-beings/EA5E36B2C5D64AE648FD1D993E6F92CC), [Laterpress on the composition and use of series bibles](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/), [Figments and Fables on series bibles for novels and television](https://figmentsandfables.com/2022/03/29/step-by-step-guide-to-writing-a-series-bible-for-television-and-novels-part-i-of-iii/)

## Minimal departure {#minimal-departure}

Do not restate defaults already supplied by reality and genre convention; record where the work differs. Before a downstream layer depends on a departure, make its scope and consequences explicit.

Where no departure is declared, the reader's known default remains. Do not quietly change a default for later convenience and present the change as a reversal.

This item asks only about the boundary between real-world defaults and work-specific canon. The settings canon-coverage-map obligation governs whether every needed domain has been assessed.

Sources: [Ryan's principle of minimal departure](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-422X%2880%2990030-3), [Ryan on the construction of fictional worlds](https://archive.org/details/possibleworldsar0000ryan)

## Internal coherence {#internal-coherence}

Make facts within and across files fit one world. Directly compare and calculate timelines, ages, distances, travel time, units, money, capacity, resource flow, and authority.

When a contradiction appears, repair the earliest setting or record an explicit change. Where the truth is a range, use that range instead of false precision.

Sources: [Cambridge scholarship on coherence of distance and temporal scale](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/scale-crisis-and-the-modern-novel/introduction/2DC3BA9BA9CF6C3DE80AD1A2A52FFDCB), [Laterpress on the series bible as a continuity record](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/), [Figments and Fables on series bibles for novels and television](https://figmentsandfables.com/2022/03/29/step-by-step-guide-to-writing-a-series-bible-for-television-and-novels-part-i-of-iii/)
