# Settings Principles

Criteria for settings files that establish the work's facts and constraints.

## Fact status {#fact-status}

Distinguish whether each fact is verified by external sources, decided for the work, disputed, or not yet decided. Do not present an inference or provisional choice as settled fact.

Mark an unresolved matter as unresolved and record its decision condition. This item governs only the fact's standing; source support governs source quality.

Sources: [Cambridge scholarship on recorded fact and invention](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75), [Jane Friedman on separating fact and fiction in historical novels](https://janefriedman.com/dos-and-donts-of-writing-historical-fiction/), [Laterpress on the series bible as a continuity record](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/)

## Source support {#source-support}

For externally verifiable claims about history, law, medicine, science, or culture, connect support from primary material, official records, scholarship, and critical editions whenever available. Record the timing, bias, scope, and conflicting conclusions of sources.

Do not invent precision the sources do not support. Do not dress a fact decided for the work in external authority; label it as a decision.

Sources: [Cambridge scholarship on recorded fact and invention](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-journal-of-postcolonial-literary-inquiry/article/postcolonialism-and-the-historical-novel-epistemologies-of-contemporary-realism/8393005CD507C40B812170BC31E37C75), [Jane Friedman on historical-fiction research](https://janefriedman.com/dos-and-donts-of-writing-historical-fiction/), [Novlr on researching history for fiction](https://www.novlr.org/the-reading-room/how-to-research-and-bring-authentic-history-to-your-fiction)

## Constraint sufficiency {#constraint-sufficiency}

State the limits, costs, exceptions, authority, resources, and dependencies of adopted rules far enough that lower layers need not invent essential conditions. Sufficiency is measured not by length but by whether later choices can be judged.

When a value cannot yet be fixed, record an allowed range or decision condition. Do not hide a blank and leave its interpretation downstream.

This item asks whether an adopted constraint is complete. Minimal departure governs which real-world defaults need to be recorded at all.

Sources: [Ronen on the necessary incompleteness of fictional worlds](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/possible-worlds-in-literary-theory/fictional-entities-incomplete-beings/EA5E36B2C5D64AE648FD1D993E6F92CC), [Laterpress on the contents and use of a series bible](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/), [Figments and Fables on series bibles for television and novels](https://figmentsandfables.com/2022/03/29/step-by-step-guide-to-writing-a-series-bible-for-television-and-novels-part-i-of-iii/)

## Minimal departure {#minimal-departure}

Do not repeat defaults already supplied by reality and genre convention; record where the work departs from them. Before a lower layer depends on a departure, make its scope and consequences clear.

Where no difference is declared, the defaults readers know remain in force. Do not quietly change a default for later convenience and present the change as a reversal.

Sources: [Ryan's principle of minimal departure](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-422X%2880%2990030-3), [Ryan on constructing fictional worlds](https://archive.org/details/possibleworldsar0000ryan)

## Internal coherence {#internal-coherence}

Make facts within and across files interlock as one world. Directly compare and calculate chronology, ages, distance, travel time, units, currency, capacity, resource flows, and authority.

When facts conflict, repair the earliest setting or record an explicit change. Where truth is a range, use the range instead of false precision.

Sources: [Cambridge scholarship on consistency of distance, time, and scale](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/scale-crisis-and-the-modern-novel/introduction/2DC3BA9BA9CF6C3DE80AD1A2A52FFDCB), [Laterpress on the series bible as a continuity record](https://www.laterpress.com/craft-of-writing/series-bible/), [Figments and Fables on series bibles for television and novels](https://figmentsandfables.com/2022/03/29/step-by-step-guide-to-writing-a-series-bible-for-television-and-novels-part-i-of-iii/)
