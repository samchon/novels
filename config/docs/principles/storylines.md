# Storyline Principles

Principles for the layer that owns causes, choices, events, and changes. No item may be excluded; each item binds wherever its condition applies, and citing it asserts that compliance. Where a file holds no unit an item governs, the citation states that scope fact.

## Figure in event {#figure-in-event}

When a unit contains a meaningful choice, make intelligible which of the chooser's current desires, values, knowledge, relationships, or pressures it comes from; not acting, hesitating, complying, and watching are themselves choices whose consequences are accepted. A choice against established disposition needs the situation or shift of awareness that made it possible, and no figure is used merely as a vehicle for events the author decreed.

Disposition and state may be stated directly as needed, but action, speech, thought, gesture, and effect on others must concretize or complicate that statement, and the agreement or gap between what a figure claims about itself and what is observed is a deliberate choice. A figure may change or refuse to change; either way its responses to experience and pressure stay intelligible without contradicting what was established. Units in which no figure chooses or is shown do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Purdue OWL on characters and desire](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction-basics/characters_and_fiction_writing.html), [Oregon State University on characterization](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-characterization-literature-definition-and-examples), [reading of character goal shifts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4266429/)

## Dramatic tension {#dramatic-tension}

A unit that intends tension holds a question the reader grips, a difference between possible outcomes, and an uncertainty that keeps the outcome unsettled. Scale and kind of risk follow what the figures or the narration hold important, not the genre's default stakes. Modulate pressure in ways fitted to the work: rising cost, shrinking options, waiting, information gaps, internal contradiction.

Units not aimed at tension — transition, recovery, meditation, atmosphere — do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Open University on suspense](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/creative-writing/start-writing-fiction/content-section-3.4), [UC San Diego on conflict, crisis, and foreshadowing](https://extendedstudies.ucsd.edu/news-events/extended-studies-blog/the-call-to-creative-writing-finding-your-voice-telling-your-story)

## Action and consequence {#action-consequence}

Write narratively weighted action not as a list of motions but as a process linking the actor's intention, the given conditions, the response, and the result. Match the size and kind of consequence to the weight the work gives the action, and leave behind what actually changed among body, information, resources, relationships, perception, and emotion.

Do not let status or ability from the settings decide success automatically; let situation and choice operate. Units that handle no action or consequence do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Purdue OWL on goals and conflict](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction-basics/writing_compelling_characters.html), [BubbleCow on scene structure: goal, conflict, and outcome](https://bubblecow.com/blog/writing-craft/pacing-and-scenes/scene-structure-goal-conflict-and-outcome/)

## Disclosure {#disclosure}

Distinguish what the reader needs to follow the current narration from what is deliberately withheld. Do not dump names, rules, relationships, and time shifts at once; introduce new information attached to context already given, and ground in advance any condition decisive for a later outcome without letting the grounding announce what it grounds — a planting that explains its own payoff spends the discovery early and leaves the later unit nothing to perform.

A planting works naturally in its own moment as action, object, speech, or image while opening a later possibility; development shifts the cue's risk or reading instead of repeating it; payoff connects the earlier detail to a new meaning, producing surprise and recognition together. Never stress a hint like a core promise when no payoff is planned, and never let a core resolution depend on a rule never planted. A revelation extends or legitimately revises earlier facts rather than voiding them, and leaves behind a change in judgement, relationship, situation, or interpretation. Units that neither plant, develop, nor disclose do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Brewer & Lichtenstein's structural-affect theory](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00017568), [Oregon State University on foreshadowing](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-foreshadowing), [University of Minnesota on plot, suspense, and surprise](https://manifold.open.umn.edu/read/c10-plot-rodak-storey/section/d89285f2-a958-4caa-9c4d-7f0f612724b8)

## Scene function {#scene-function}

Every scene owns a function among event, recognition, relationship, emotion, atmosphere, image, rhythm, or reader interpretation. An action scene uses goal, obstruction, choice, and result as needed; a scene without people can function through environment, traces, absence, and the arrangement of sensation; minimal change and stillness are possible.

What the scene's duration adds to its neighbours and to the whole must be explainable. Two scenes that discharge the same function without new effect are one scene: merge them, or cut the redundant one.

Sources: [BubbleCow on scene structure: goal, conflict, and outcome](https://bubblecow.com/blog/writing-craft/pacing-and-scenes/scene-structure-goal-conflict-and-outcome/), [Fiction University on what qualifies as a scene](http://blog.janicehardy.com/2019/08/how-to-write-scenes-and-what-qualifies.html)

## Resolution and aftermath {#resolution-aftermath}

A unit responsible for closure chooses which of the work's built-up questions, relationships, images, and formal promises it closes and which it leaves open.

A closed ending makes its outcome intelligible from earlier conditions and choices; an open ending provides the meaning of not answering and the grounds on which the reader will judge. The closing weight of each major element follows the weight the work accumulated in it. Make identifiable what changed after the end, or what finally did not change. Units with no closing function do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Texas A&M University Writing Center on plot and resolution](https://writingcenter.tamu.edu/guides/resources/analyzing-novels.html), [Southeastern Louisiana University's ending review questions](https://www.southeastern.edu/wp-content/uploads/omni-misc-files/acad_research/programs/writing_center/handouts/pdf_handouts/readingandwritingabo.pdf)

## Thematic coherence {#thematic-coherence}

Theme emerges from the repetitions and differences made by events, figures, relationships, places, images, voice, and form, not from a correct answer appended from outside the work.

The work may aim at one position or hold several interpretations open, but its chosen elements must connect to a central concern instead of scattering into unrelated claims. Symbols and recurring images keep, extend, or invert their meaning by context while still carrying their scene's own function. Units with no thematic responsibility do not trigger this item.

Sources: [Texas A&M University Writing Center on theme](https://writingcenter.tamu.edu/guides/resources/analyzing-novels.html), [UW–Madison Writing Center on cohesion](https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/style/connectingideas/)
