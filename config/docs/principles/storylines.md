# Storyline Principles

Criteria for storyline files that establish connections and changes or accumulations among events, relationships, and formal elements.

## Treatment paragraphs {#treatment-paragraphs}

The direct body of each H2, H3, and H4 first gives a brief orientation to the inherited state, central pressure or formal task, change within the unit, and direction of exit; connected detailed paragraphs then develop each causal or logical phase. The direct body of an H2 or H3 organizes the cumulative movement of its descendants without repeating them or narrating a hidden scene in their place.

Each paragraph owns one cause, attempt, counteraction, choice, discovery, aftermath, or inseparable progression bundle. Split when the function changes; join disconnected fragments or strengthen their transition. Do not judge by fixed paragraph or character counts, and do not let the brief orientation become a duplicate summary of the detailed development.

This item asks about the treatment's information arrangement. The common substantive-completion obligation governs sufficient development of events and effects, and narrative unit addressability governs actual unit boundaries.

Sources: [Purdue OWL on developing paragraphs from a general statement through concrete support](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/resources/teaching_resources/documents/the-writing-process-20250724.pdf), [George Mason University on reverse outlining paragraph functions and order](https://writingcenter.gmu.edu/writing-resources/writing-as-process/reverse-outlining)

## Information design {#information-design}

For every unit, decide what the reader knows, does not yet know, and is invited to ask; when misdirection is used, decide what the reader is led to believe and why. Develop major revelations from earlier planted facts so surprise does not invalidate what came before.

Later answer, transform, or deliberately leave open the questions and promises established earlier. Do not manufacture a secret for a work that does not use suspense or reversal.

This item asks about the order of revelation across the work. Narrative information entry governs whether information is intelligible when it first appears, and scenario knowledge state governs knowledge held by each participant within a scene.

Sources: [Barthes on the sequence of question, delay, and answer](https://archive.org/details/sz0000bart), [Brewer and Lichtenstein on information order, suspense, curiosity, and surprise](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00017568), [Oregon State University on foreshadowing](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-foreshadowing)

## Resolution and aftermath {#resolution-aftermath}

Closing units decide which accumulated questions, choices, relationships, and images close and which remain open. The result must be intelligible from earlier conditions and leave what changed, or finally did not change, after the ending.

An open ending still gives the reader grounds for judgment and a reason to stop. Do not require universal resolution or an epilogue.

This item asks how the ending handles prior accumulation. The storyline ending-position obligation governs which H2 is actually last in the current delivery unit.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the range from open to closed endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC), [Purdue OWL on the relation among plot, climax, resolution, and theme](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/writing_in_literature/writing_in_literature_detailed_discussion/reading_a_novel_or_story.html)

## Thematic development {#thematic-development}

Let the work's central concerns emerge through repetition and difference in events, choices, relationships, places, images, and form. Do not append an external explanation that supplies the answer.

The work may take one position or permit several interpretations. In either case, recurring elements must not repeat the same meaning unchanged; let them persist or transform in new contexts.

Sources: [UNC Writing Center on meaning emerging through form and detail in fiction](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/literature-fiction/), [Magliano and colleagues on the relation between character-goal tracking and thematic understanding](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4266429/)
