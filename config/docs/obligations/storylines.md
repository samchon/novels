# Storyline Obligations

## Reader entry {#reader-entry}

Assign the first sequence through which the reader enters the work. State what the reader first perceives and which character, voice, context, pressure, or form gives reason to read the next unit.

The work may begin in medias res, with exposition, an image, a document, or another form. Make the chosen beginning perform an entry function specific to this work.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the multiple functions of beginnings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC), [Oregon State University on the position and function of prologues](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-prologue)

## Ending position {#ending-position}

Assign the last sequence through which the reader leaves the current delivery. State why the work stops here and what state, question, emotion, or image it leaves last.

This item asks for the actual final sequence. Settings delivery-scope obligation governs the boundary of the delivered work, and storyline resolution and aftermath governs what is closed and left open.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the range from open to closed endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC), [Richardson on the distinction between ending and closure](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61883/chapter-abstract/547759132)

## Prologue role {#prologue}

A separate unit preceding the main progression establishes the earlier time, voice, context, question, or image needed before the main work, and creates a reason to cross into the first main unit.

Do not detach exposition that the main work should carry or add atmosphere unconnected to later events.

Sources: [Oregon State University on the position and function of prologues](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-prologue), [Burton and Poster on the multiple functions of beginnings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC)

## Epilogue role {#epilogue}

A separate unit after the main resolution reveals persistence, change, distance, or a later time position visible only after the ending, and thereby changes the work's final meaning.

Do not repeat a resolution already complete or empty the current work's ending merely to advertise another work.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on how endings land and where a work stops](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings-tension-and-pace/7B84CB546C696FD1B6A8AB753522B42C), [Richardson on the distinction between ending and closure](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61883/chapter-abstract/547759132)
