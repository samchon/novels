# Storyline Obligations

The reader-entry and ending positions that the complete population of storyline H2 sequences must define.

## Reader entry {#reader-entry}

Define the first sequence through which the reader enters the work. State what the reader first perceives and which character, voice, context, pressure, or formal device prepares the reading of the next unit.

The work may begin in medias res, with exposition, an image, a document, or another form. Make the chosen opening perform an entry function specific to this work.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the several functions of beginnings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC), [Oregon State University on the placement and function of prologues](https://liberalarts.oregonstate.edu/wlf/what-prologue)

## Ending position {#ending-position}

Define the final sequence from which the reader leaves this delivery unit. State why the work stops there and which state, question, emotion, or image it leaves last.

This item asks for the actual final sequence. The settings delivery-scope obligation governs the deliverable's extent, and the storyline resolution-and-aftermath principle governs what closes and what remains open.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the range from open to closed endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC), [Richardson on the distinction between ending and closure](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61883/chapter-abstract/547759132)
