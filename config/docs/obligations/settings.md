# Settings Obligations

Roles that the complete population of settings H2 units must define.

## Delivery scope {#delivery-scope}

Define the work this package will complete and deliver now. State its volume or serial length, beginning and ending, included and excluded intervals, degree of completion, and relationship to any larger work.

This item asks for the boundary of the deliverable. The storyline ending-position obligation governs the actual final sequence and its function.

Sources: [Furneaux on provisional closure in continuing narrative](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/virtual-play-and-the-victorian-novel/continuation-attachment-and-william-makepeace-thackeray/85BD61006C84A23DF2563A6993771323), [Burton and Poster on the relation among beginnings, endings, form, and length](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/the-book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/BCFED30FA31DEC5A505CDE80AC6C5675)

## Work promise {#work-promise}

Define the central experience the work intends to give its reader. State which question, emotion, understanding, pleasure, or formal effect it will develop to the end and how that promise governs the work's choices.

This does not force one theme or conclusion. When several effects are used, make clear what they jointly serve.

Sources: [Jakobson on the dominant that governs other elements](https://archive.org/details/readingsinrussia0000mate), [Burton and Poster on testing the function of chapters and how a work lands](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings-tension-and-pace/7B84CB546C696FD1B6A8AB753522B42C)

## Reader access {#reader-access}

Define how the reader accesses events and characters. Record the narrator, focalization, psychic distance, reliability, available information, and conditions for perspective changes wherever the work uses them.

A work without a fixed narrator or focal subject instead defines the authority and transition rules of documents, dialogue, observation, or its other delivery modes.

Sources: [Genette on distinguishing voice from focalization](https://archive.org/details/narrativediscour0000gene), [the Living Handbook of Narratology on theories of the narrator and the possibility of narratorless narrative](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/44.html), [Yeazell on perspective as sentence-level technique in English prose fiction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-prose/perspective/D6757DF651808258ADF5959AD523B24A), [Burton and Poster on viewpoint choice and transitions](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/point-of-view/9E9D1AA8E2841E6508D5864791DDE0FE)

## Canon coverage map {#canon-coverage-map}

Define the work's settings domains and the file families that own each one. Audit world laws; time and history; space and environment; agents and relationships; groups, institutions, and power; society, culture, language, and belief; material life, economy, and infrastructure; knowledge, technology, and capabilities; cross-system relations and opening state; and sources, disputes, and uncertainty. Classify each domain as explicit canon, inherited real-world default, outside scope, or unresolved.

This map is not a closed checklist. When a new domain emerges, add or reorganize the appropriate file family and update the map. The settings addressable-canon principle governs decomposition into H2 owners, the common scope-preservation obligation governs missing delivery scope, and substantive completion governs the depth of each unit.

For an unresolved domain, record the decision condition and research path. If downstream work depends on it, the unresolved label is not completed coverage; stop downstream progress until the needed canon is settled.

Sources: [Marie-Laure Ryan on storyworlds as spacetime containing existents, events, and state changes](https://marilaur.info/degrees.pdf), [Mark J. P. Wolf on maps, timelines, genealogies, nature, culture, language, myth, philosophy, and their relations as world infrastructure](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-imaginary-worlds/9781136220807/xhtml/12_Chapter03.xhtml), [OpenStax Anthropology on interactions among social domains including economy, politics, religion, and family](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-anthropology/pages/1-1-the-study-of-humanity-or-anthropology-is-vast)
