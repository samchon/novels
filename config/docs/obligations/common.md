# Common Unit Obligations

Every settings H2 and every storyline, scenario, and manuscript H2, H3, and H4 answers these obligations directly. Judge the current unit and its descendants, not the work on average. The four items ask only about omission from declared scope, completion at the unit's own layer, scale and allocation, and the truth of evidence statements.

## Scope preservation {#scope-preservation}

The current unit fully realizes the part it owns of the delivery scope and work promise fixed in settings. Across the complete unit population, every declared beginning and ending, required interval, character, event, constraint, relationship, and consequence has an owner. Omitting required material entirely or leaving only a title is a scope loss.

This item asks only what must be included. Proportionate development governs how many units and how much authored body develop the included material.

When this obligation fails, do not cite it or record it as reviewed. Stop the current stage transition and downstream authorship, restore the missing scope at its earliest owning layer, and realign every descendant. If the scope itself must shrink, obtain user approval and revise the settings delivery scope first.

Sources: [NASA on complete requirement sets and bidirectional traceability](https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/7150/pages/16450593/SWE-051%2B-%2BSoftware%2BRequirements%2BAnalysis), [NASA on unique identification and one requirement per statement](https://swehb.nasa.gov/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=146540037), [Detailed Outline Control on hierarchical detail and downstream alignment for long-form coherence](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.190/), [ttsc on independent complete coverage between requirements and implementation populations](https://ttsc.dev/docs/evidence/claims/)

## Substantive completion {#substantive-completion}

The current unit is a complete deliverable at its own layer. A settings H2 settles the boundary, operating conditions, and downstream consequences of the fact or constraint it owns so they can be used without guesswork. A storyline unit is a detailed treatment whose chosen narrative connection and changed or accumulated effect need not be invented by the next layer. A scenario unit is a detailed initial script that fixes action or process, reaction, movement, required speech or silence, and exit state in executable order within time and space. A manuscript unit is finished literary prose for a reader.

A title, promise of future writing, event list, causal card, restatement of a parent unit, field label, or few-line summary is not a layer deliverable. Storyline and scenario H2/H3 bodies directly establish their scope's question or organizing principle, cumulative progression, and end state or effect. Manuscript H2/H3 units are realized by the complete finished prose of their descendants and do not add planning summaries to the work.

This item asks only whether the unit is an actual deliverable. Proportionate development governs the required unit count and allocation of page space among units.

When this obligation fails, do not add a tag; rewrite the unit. If downstream work would need to invent a core decision, strengthen the earliest owning layer. If a local edit cannot restore completion, rewrite the unit from a correct design and reread every affected unit.

Sources: [Detailed Outline Control on moving creative burden upstream and controlling drafts through detailed outlines](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.190/), [Purdue OWL on the distinct functions of scene and summary](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/resources/writing_tutors/tutoring_creative_writing_students/documents/working-with-creative-writing-students-09152025.pdf), [BBC on scene headings, screen action, action beats, and dialogue as actual script elements](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/writersroom/scripts/screenplay.pdf)

## Proportionate development {#proportionate-development}

Across the complete population, unit count and granularity, authored body within each unit, and internal progression are proportionate to the declared work scale and to the importance of events, relationships, and formal elements. Do not compress the major periods, conflicts, turns, or formal accumulations of a long or multi-volume work into a few umbrella units or short treatments, and do not inflate minor intervals merely to fill total length. Compare sibling units and adjacent layers, assigning more concrete development and consequence to important material.

When the user specifies volume count, serial length, life span, or historical interval, that scale governs. When rewriting the same scope, the earlier version's files, H2/H3/H4 counts, authored body excluding comments, distribution by unit, and research breadth are regression alarms. Counts do not prove quality, but a result in which several measures collapse together without an approved scope change is not complete.

This item asks only about the quantity and allocation of development. Scope preservation governs whether required content exists, and substantive completion governs whether each unit is a deliverable at its layer.

When this obligation fails, redesign the complete unit allocation and granularity, expand important units to the needed depth, or separate wrongly bundled scope. Stop the stage transition and downstream authorship until proportion is restored across one complete first version.

Sources: [CONCOCT on over-summary of important events and over-development of minor events as long-form pacing failures](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.723/), [Hicks on distance, time, and proportion as organizers of novelistic progression](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/scale-crisis-and-the-modern-novel/introduction/2DC3BA9BA9CF6C3DE80AD1A2A52FFDCB), [ACL research on evaluating long-form output across independent dimensions including completeness and factuality](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.181/)

## Evidence-content conformance {#evidence-content-conformance}

Every acknowledgement, exclusion, and review in the current unit states only a relationship that actually holds between the complete host and target. A passing compiler, absence of diagnostics, and valid fingerprint do not substitute for semantic agreement. Each reason names how the host realizes the concrete fact, constraint, development, or effect required by the target.

Citing an unrelated or semantically bundled target, excluding missing work, using a generic or copied reason or review, inventing or refreshing an unchecked fingerprint, weakening a stage, config, population, or path to remove a diagnostic, or moving content to evade the intended claim is a violation. Adding prose that merely repeats the target's wording is not realization.

When this obligation fails, do not write or retain the statement. Stop the current evidence batch and downstream work. Follow the `$evidence-graph` diagnostic procedure: read the complete host, target, and context; enumerate and compare plausible causes and repair paths; then fix the earliest actual owner. When meaning, ownership, structure, or content is wrong, the permitted repair ranges from splitting, moving, deleting, researching, or rewriting an H2 through discarding and rebuilding the affected file or layer. Propagate every consequence and reread literally before resuming the statement or the work.

This item asks only about the truth of evidence statements and their repair. Scope preservation governs whether required scope exists, substantive completion governs the unit's content, and proportionate development governs quantity and allocation.
